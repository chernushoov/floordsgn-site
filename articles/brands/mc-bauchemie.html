MC-Bauchemie — German Industrial Resin

May 2026 · 6 min read

MC-Bauchemie is the German industrial resin floor manufacturer that competes with Sika's mid-tier epoxy SL spec at meaningful price savings. The MC-DUR product family covers 100%-solids epoxy SL, heavy-duty PU industrial, and rapid-cure systems with KineticBoost technology. For IL specifiers looking for German engineering at a price below Sika's premium, MC-Bauchemie's confirmed channel via A.Z Marketing Ltd in Ramle closes the procurement question.

The product range

MC-DUR 1320 VK (transparent epoxy)
2–3 mm transparent epoxy SL for car-park and industrial applications. Standard 100%-solids two-component formulation. Used as primer-and-finish on warehouse floors and structural decks. Public TDS PDF.
MC-DUR PowerCoat (heavy-duty industrial)
Heavy-duty industrial coating rated for chemical and mechanical exposure to 120°C. Direct competitor to Sikafloor 263 SL HC (high-chemical) and Mapefloor I 302 SL at the heavy-industrial end of epoxy SL. Product page.
MC-DUR TopSpeed SC (rapid-cure)
KineticBoost technology — rapid-cure SC variant with accelerated walking-traffic and return-to-service times. Used for retrofit projects with shutdown-cost constraints. Public TDS PDF.

Where MC-Bauchemie wins

  • German engineering at sub-Sika premium. MC-DUR PowerCoat and 1320 VK compete with Sikafloor 263 SL on parameters at typically lower line-item cost. For projects where Sika's channel premium is unjustified by the use case, MC-Bauchemie closes the spec without sacrificing engineering quality.
  • KineticBoost rapid-cure niche. TopSpeed SC's accelerated cure addresses retrofit and shutdown-cost-sensitive projects. Sika's MMA-grade 403/404 is faster but with significant install odour; KineticBoost is closer to MMA cure speed without the methacrylate-smell penalty.
  • Comprehensive certification stack. CE EN 13813, CE EN 1504-2, AgBB low-emission, ISO 9001/14001 — full public documentation for IL public-sector tender language.
  • Confirmed IL channel. A.Z Marketing in Ramle is documented on the manufacturer's worldwide locations page. Procurement-team verification is straightforward — call the distributor directly.

Where MC-Bauchemie loses

  • Brand recognition vs Sika in IL. Despite German-engineering credentials, MC-Bauchemie does not have the IL tender-language reflex that Sika has. Specifying MC-Bauchemie requires architect or engineer-of-record advocacy beyond brand-name reflex.
  • No PU-cement engineering equivalent to Sikafloor PurCem. The MC range is epoxy-centric. For heavy industrial PU-cement (food production, brewery), see PU-cement Big-3 — MC-Bauchemie is not in that competitive set.
  • Applicator network depth in IL. A.Z Marketing's distribution is real but applicator-pool size compared to Gilar (Sika's IL distributor) is unverified. For projects requiring large simultaneous applicator deployment, verify before tender close.

Final read

MC-Bauchemie is the right specification for IL industrial epoxy SL projects where German engineering matters but Sika's premium does not justify itself. Confirmed channel via A.Z Marketing in Ramle removes the procurement risk. For heavy industrial PU-cement spec, look at Sika PurCem, Ucrete, or Flowfresh; MC-Bauchemie's strength is epoxy SL across light-to-medium industrial duty. Call the IL distributor directly to confirm exact product range coverage before tender drafting.
