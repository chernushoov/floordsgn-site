Industry Resources for Floor Specifiers

Maintained list · updated May 2026

The standards bodies, trade associations, and technical publications behind every credible floor specification. Where Floor.DSGN reads, what we cite, and what every spec-writer working at a serious level should bookmark. Curated for IL-market relevance, EU/US authority, and decorative + industrial floor span. Standards glossary defines the short codes used throughout.

Standards Bodies and Authoritative Documents

The technical specifications that flooring warranties reference. Cite these by clause; do not paraphrase from memory.

USA · Concrete Repair

ICRI — International Concrete Repair Institute

Owner of CSP 1–10 surface profile system. Publishes 310.2R-2013 (selecting and specifying concrete surface preparation), 320.5R (sealing concrete), and other guidelines referenced by every coating manufacturer's warranty.

icri.org
UK · Resin Floors

FeRFA — UK Resin Flooring Association

Publishes the FeRFA Type 1–8 classification — the European reference taxonomy for resin floor systems by thickness and use class. Free downloadable guides for spec-writers.

ferfa.org.uk
EU · Screeds Master Standard

EN 13813 — Screed materials

The European master standard for floor screeds. Classifies binder families as CT (cementitious), CA (calcium sulfate), MA (magnesite), SR (synthetic resin), AS (mastic asphalt). Mandatory citation in any EU floor spec.

EN 13813 at DIN Media
EU · Repair Mortars

EN 1504 (parts 1–10) — Concrete repair

Ten-part standard covering surface protection (-2), structural repair (-3), general principles (-9). Cite for every repair-mortar and protective-coating specification on existing concrete.

CEN-CENELEC
USA · Slab Construction

ACI 302.1R — Floor and Slab Construction

The American Concrete Institute's guide to placing, finishing, and curing concrete floor slabs. Companion document ACI 360 covers slabs-on-ground. Both are the design-side reference for industrial floors.

ACI 302.1R store
USA · Moisture Testing

ASTM F2170 — In-Situ RH Probes

The standard method for relative-humidity testing of concrete slabs using in-situ probes at 40% slab depth. The accepted gating test before resin coating application. Companion F1869 covers calcium chloride emission rate.

ASTM F2170
USA · Adhesion Testing

ASTM D7234 / EN 1542 — Pull-Off

The destructive test for coating adhesion to concrete. Required acceptance test under most manufacturer warranties before sign-off. DeFelsko PosiTest AT is the standard field instrument.

ASTM D7234
USA · Silica Safety

OSHA 29 CFR §1926.1153 — Respirable Silica

Construction silica exposure rule. Table 1 dictates HEPA vacuum + respirator combinations for grinding, shot-blasting, and cutting concrete. Non-negotiable on US and IL sites for liability cover.

OSHA silica rule
USA · Slip Resistance

ANSI A326.3 / EN 16165 — DCOF / PTV

Standard methods for floor slip resistance: ANSI A326.3 measures Dynamic Coefficient of Friction (DCOF); EN 16165 covers Pendulum Test Value (PTV). Specify ≥ 0.42 DCOF or ≥ 36 PTV for wet barefoot areas.

TCNA on ANSI A326.3
EU · Sports + Playground

EN 14904 + EN 1177 — Sports + Playground

EN 14904 classifies indoor sports floors as Point-Elastic, Area-Elastic, Combined-Elastic. EN 1177 sets playground impact attenuation by Critical Fall Height. Both gate the warranty on poured-PU rubber floors.

CEN standards portal
Israel · National Standards

SII — Standards Institution of Israel (ת״י)

Official body for ת״י (Israeli) standards. Direct references: ת״י 466 (concrete construction), ת״י 1923 (slip resistance), ת״י 5566 (waterproofing in wet rooms). Authoritative for any IL public-sector spec.

sii.org.il
Israel · Government Spec

המפרט הבין־משרדי (Inter-Ministerial Spec)

The Hebrew "Blue Book" — binding finishes spec for government construction in Israel. Section 11 covers floors; Sections 09 + 10 cover screeds and waterproofing. Reference for tendering on public projects.

gov.il portal

Trade Associations

Industry bodies that publish position statements, training, and member directories.

USA · Flatwork Contractors

ASCC — American Society of Concrete Contractors

Position statements on flatwork construction, finishing, cracking. Practical contractor-side complement to ACI's design-side documents.

ascconline.org
USA · Industrial Coatings

AMPP (formerly SSPC) — Materials Protection

Industrial protective-coatings authority. SSPC-SP 13 / NACE No. 6 (concrete surface preparation) is referenced alongside ICRI 310.2R for coating specifications.

ampp.org
Germany · Concrete

DBV — Deutscher Beton- und Bautechnik-Verein

German concrete authority. Publishes DBV merkblätter (technical leaflets) widely used on industrial-floor projects in EU central markets.

betonverein.de
EU · Adhesives + Sealants

FEICA — Adhesive & Sealant Industry

European trade body for adhesives and sealants. Sets standards used in tile-over-resin and waterproofing-membrane assemblies under flooring.

feica.eu
Israel · Builders

ACB — Israel Builders Association (התאחדות הקבלנים)

Apex builders' body; lobbies on regulations, publishes contractor-facing materials. Member directory is the starting point for vetting IL flooring contractors.

acb.org.il
Israel · Engineers

AEAI — Association of Engineers, Architects & Graduates

Engineering-side authority on slabs and repair. Cite when defending an IL slab spec against builder cost-cuts.

aeai.org.il

Manufacturer Technical Portals

Where the actual product specs live. Always read the regional TDS — IL distribution may differ from EU/US.

Switzerland · Coatings

Sika Flooring

Sikafloor product family: Sikafloor PurCem (PU-cement), DecoDur (decorative), Comfortfloor (residential PU), 263 SL (epoxy SL). Full TDS library, regional filtering.

sika.com flooring
Italy · Coatings

Mapei Mapefloor

Mapefloor I 302 SL, PU 410, CPU/HD (PU-cement). Bioblock silver-ion antimicrobial line is the brand differentiator in healthcare and food production.

Mapefloor lines
USA/Germany · Coatings

MasterTop (Master Builders Solutions)

Former BASF Construction Chemicals (now MBCC Group, owned by Sika since 2023). Ucrete UD200 — the PU-cement gold standard. MasterTop 1235SL, 1817PC, 1912 Xolutec for industrial floor systems.

master-builders-solutions.com
UK · PU-Cement

Flowcrete — Flowfresh / Flowshield

Owned by Tremco CPG. Flowfresh PU-cement with embedded Polygiene silver-ion (mirror to Mapei Bioblock). Flowshield SL epoxy SL, Mondéco terrazzo, Deckshield car park PU.

flowcrete.com
USA · Resin Floors

Stonhard — Stonclad / Stonshield / Stonchem

Unique single-source warranty model — material + installation + warranty all from one company. Stonclad UT (PU-cement), Stonshield (epoxy with grit), Stonchem (chemical-resistant).

stonhard.com
Spain · Microcement

Topciment

Spanish microcement reference. Sttandard (2-component), Evoluttion (1-component), Atlanttic (pool-grade), Industtrial (epoxy-microcement). Full TDS library in EN/ES/FR/IT.

topciment.com
Belgium · Microcement

Mortex by Beal International

Cement-lime-polymer hybrid, water-repellent in the mass. Reference product for shower and bathroom installations without separate membrane on stable substrates.

bealinternational.com
UK · Safety Vinyl

Altro / Polyflor (safety vinyl)

Altro Stronghold 30, Polyflor Polysafe Apex — the two UK-origin safety vinyl benchmarks for healthcare kitchens, wet labs, food prep. Lifetime PTV warranty is the Altro brand-unique claim.

altro.com · polyflor.com

Trade Press

Where industry news, new-product launches, and contractor case studies are published.

USA · Decorative

Concrete Decor Magazine

The most-cited US decorative-concrete publication. Stamped, stained, polished, overlay coverage. Contractor profiles. Product database.

concretedecor.net
USA · Industrial

Concrete Construction Magazine

US industrial/commercial concrete trade magazine. Covers flatwork, placement, surface prep, repair from the contractor's angle.

concreteconstruction.net
USA · Coatings Industry

Coatings World / JPCL

Coatings World covers industrial coatings monthly. JPCL (Journal of Protective Coatings & Linings) covers protective and concrete coatings — strong on surface prep and ICRI alignment.

coatingsworld.com · paintsquare.com
UK · Resin Floors

Resin Flooring Magazine (FeRFA-linked)

UK industry-aligned editorial output for resin floors. Whitepapers, spec guides, member project case studies. Reads as an extension of FeRFA's technical library.

ferfa.org.uk/resources
USA · Consumer + Contractor

The Concrete Network

Long-running consumer-and-contractor portal since 1999. Strong on stamped, polished, overlay. Cost guides. Contractor directory. SEO-friendly for inbound citations.

concretenetwork.com
Israel · Architecture

Architecture of Israel Quarterly (AI)

Hebrew/English bilingual since 1988. The most credible IL design-press citation venue for serious finishes work.

aiqmag.com
