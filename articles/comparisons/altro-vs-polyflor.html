Altro vs Polyflor — UK Safety Vinyl Head-to-Head

May 2026 · 8 min read

Altro Stronghold 30 and Polyflor Polysafe Apex 55 are the two UK calendered safety vinyl flagships. Both target the same buyer envelope: commercial kitchens, hospital wet zones, food production, wet labs — environments where slip-fall liability dominates the spec. On paper they look nearly equivalent: 3.0 mm calendered vinyl, R12 slip class, PUR-enhanced surface. The differences emerge in the warranty language, range depth, and price-per-m². This article maps those differences honestly.

The headline difference — warranty language

Altro's lifetime PTV ≥ 36 warranty is the strongest single claim in the entire safety-vinyl category. Polyflor's PTV ≥ 36 sustained warranty is real and credible, but does not match the lifetime language in legal weight. Both are based on the same underlying engineering — integral aggregate in the vinyl matrix — but Altro publishes a more aggressive warranty position.

In a slip-fall claim against an Altro-floored commercial kitchen, the lifetime PTV documentation provides a defence the insurance industry recognises. In a slip-fall claim against a Polyflor-floored same kitchen, the defence is similar but the warranty wording is less unique. The legal-team variance over fifteen years of service life is real but typically small in monetary terms.

Where Altro wins

Lifetime PTV warranty

Altro is the only safety vinyl with the word "lifetime" in the slip-resistance warranty. For high-stakes liability environments (hospital wet zones, large hospitality groups, public-sector tender), this language is itself the spec value. The cost premium of 5–15% over Polyflor at equivalent product class is justified by the warranty's defensive weight.

Whiterock wall cladding integration

Altro's full wet-zone proposition includes Whiterock — a wall-cladding system designed to be heat-welded to Stronghold 30 floor at the wall-floor junction. For commercial kitchens where the entire wet zone needs a single sealed envelope, this floor-and-wall integration is unique to Altro. Polyflor does not offer an equivalent integrated wall product at parity.

NHS and public-sector brand recognition

In UK NHS hospital procurement, "Altro" is often the brand name in the tender language itself. The brand has been the reflexive default for healthcare wet-zone work for decades. In IL public-sector hospital work, the same recognition partially carries — particularly with senior procurement officers trained in the UK/Israeli healthcare system.

Where Polyflor wins

Polysafe range depth

Polyflor's Polysafe range spans ~14 named SKUs across thickness, slip class, decorative finish, and use case (Apex 55 / Standard / Verona / Hydro / Ultima / QuickLay and more). The breadth lets the specifier match exact requirements without trading across brands. For projects where the architect wants a wood-effect surface (Polysafe Verona) on a safety substrate, Altro's range does not deliver an equivalent without trading off.

Heavy-duty 3.5 mm option

Polysafe Ultima at 3.5 mm thickness handles transit zones, prison environments, and industrial wash-down zones where Stronghold 30's 3.0 mm is at the boundary. For projects requiring more wear allowance than standard, Polyflor closes the spec.

Price

Typically 85–95% of Altro at equivalent slip/thickness spec. For projects where the line-item premium of Altro is unjustified by warranty value (light commercial wet zones, education sector, retail back-of-house), Polyflor delivers the same slip protection at lower cost.

The selection sequence

  1. Is lifetime warranty language a spec requirement? NHS-style tender, large hospitality liability, healthcare insurer demand — Altro is the answer. Light commercial, education, retail — either brand works.
  2. Does the project need wall-cladding integration? Yes → Altro Whiterock + Stronghold 30 combination. No → either brand.
  3. Is a specific decorative finish (wood-effect, etc.) required? Yes → Polyflor Verona PUR range. No → either brand.
  4. Is the wear zone heavy-duty (above standard safety vinyl)? Yes → Polyflor Ultima 3.5 mm. No → either brand at 3.0 mm.
  5. Is the budget cap pushing toward 85–95% of Altro? Yes → Polyflor. No → either brand.

Common spec mistakes

  • Specifying "Altro or equivalent" but rejecting Polyflor. If the tender language says "equivalent," Polyflor Polysafe Apex 55 is the equivalent at this slip class. Rejecting it on brand-name grounds is bid-protest territory.
  • Specifying Polyflor for heavy industrial environments. Neither brand is correct for forklift / pallet-truck / hot-vessel proximity. Move to PU-cement (Flowfresh, Sikafloor PurCem).
  • Specifying 2.0 mm safety vinyl for a commercial kitchen. Both brands offer 2.0 mm variants (Polyflor Standard PUR, Altro Suprema II) but commercial kitchens demand 3.0 mm minimum for wear allowance.

IL channel — both brands need verification

Both Altro and Polyflor reach IL through specialty importers — neither has a Sika-style exclusive distributor at scale. For project tenders, phone-verify with the local importer:

  • Stock availability of the specific SKU and colour
  • Lead time (typically 4–8 weeks for non-stocked variants)
  • Approved IL applicator pool — safety vinyl installation is technique-heavy, and heat-welded seams require trained installers
  • Warranty registration process

The IL applicator pool overlaps significantly between the two brands — most installers handle both. The brand choice is therefore upstream of the install team, not downstream.

Final read

Altro is the right specification when lifetime warranty language, NHS-style brand recognition, or Whiterock wall integration matters. Polyflor is the right specification when range depth, wood-effect decorative finish, heavy-duty 3.5 mm, or 5–15% cost savings drive the decision. For most IL projects, the brands are interchangeable in functional spec — pick by which differentiator your project values, and which IL importer your team has the strongest relationship with.

Altro Stronghold 30 и Polyflor Polysafe Apex 55 — два британских флагмана calendered safety vinyl. Оба целятся в один buyer-конверт: коммерческие кухни, госпитальные мокрые зоны, food production, wet labs — среды, где slip-fall liability доминирует спеку. На бумаге выглядят почти эквивалентно: 3.0 мм calendered vinyl, R12 slip-класс, PUR-улучшенная поверхность. Разница выходит на свет в warranty-language, range-depth и price-per-m². Эта статья картирует разницу честно.

Polyflor PTV ≥ 36 sustained гарантия реальна и credible, но не matches lifetime-формулировку в legal-весе. Оба основаны на той же underlying engineering — integral aggregate в виниловой матрице — но Altro публикует более агрессивную warranty-позицию.

В slip-fall иске против Altro-floored коммерческой кухни lifetime PTV-документация даёт защиту, признаваемую insurance-индустрией. В slip-fall иске против Polyflor-floored той же кухни защита похожа, но warranty-формулировка менее уникальна. Legal-team variance за 15 лет service life реальна, но обычно мала в monetary terms.

Где Altro выигрывает

Lifetime PTV warranty

Altro — единственный safety vinyl со словом «lifetime» в slip-resistance гарантии. Для high-stakes liability сред (госпитальные мокрые зоны, large hospitality groups, public-sector тендер) эта формулировка сама — spec-value. Премия 5–15% над Polyflor на эквивалентном product class оправдана defensive-весом гарантии.

Whiterock wall-cladding integration

Полное wet-zone-предложение Altro включает Whiterock — wall-cladding систему, designed to be heat-welded к Stronghold 30 floor на wall-floor стыке. Для коммерческих кухонь, где вся wet-зона нуждается в одном sealed envelope, эта floor-and-wall integration уникальна для Altro. У Polyflor нет эквивалентного integrated wall-продукта на parity.

NHS и public-sector brand recognition

В UK NHS hospital procurement «Altro» часто — brand name в самом tender-языке. Бренд был рефлекторным дефолтом для healthcare wet-зон десятилетиями. В IL public-sector hospital работе та же recognition partly carries — особенно с senior procurement-officers, тренированными в UK/Israeli healthcare-системе.

Где Polyflor выигрывает

Polysafe range depth

Polysafe-линейка спанит ~14 named SKU через толщину, slip-класс, decorative-финиш и use case (Apex 55 / Standard / Verona / Hydro / Ultima / QuickLay и больше). Breadth позволяет спецификатору matched точные требования без trading across brands. Для проектов, где архитектор хочет wood-effect поверхность (Polysafe Verona) на safety-subстрате, Altro range не даёт эквивалента без trade-off.

Heavy-duty 3.5 мм опция

Polysafe Ultima при 3.5 мм толщине handles transit-зоны, prison-среды и industrial wash-down зоны, где Stronghold 30 3.0 мм на границе. Для проектов, требующих больше wear-allowance, Polyflor закрывает спеку.

Цена

Обычно 85–95% Altro на эквивалентной slip/thickness spec. Для проектов, где line-item премия Altro не оправдана warranty-value (lighte commercial wet-зоны, education-сектор, retail back-of-house), Polyflor доставляет ту же slip-защиту по более низкой цене.

Последовательность выбора

  1. Lifetime warranty language — spec-требование? NHS-style тендер, large hospitality liability, healthcare insurer-демand — Altro ответ. Light commercial, education, retail — оба бренда работают.
  2. Нужна wall-cladding integration? Да → Altro Whiterock + Stronghold 30. Нет → любой.
  3. Specific decorative finish (wood-effect и т.д.)? Да → Polyflor Verona PUR. Нет → любой.
  4. Heavy-duty (выше standard safety vinyl)? Да → Polyflor Ultima 3.5 мм. Нет → любой на 3.0 мм.
  5. Budget cap на 85–95% Altro? Да → Polyflor. Нет → любой.

Частые ошибки спецификации

  • «Altro or equivalent» но отказ от Polyflor. Если tender language говорит «equivalent», Polyflor Polysafe Apex 55 — эквивалент. Отказ на brand-name grounds — bid-protest territory.
  • Polyflor для тяжёлой индустрии. Ни один бренд не подходит для forklift / pallet-truck / hot-vessel proximity. Переходи на PU-cement.
  • 2.0 мм safety vinyl в коммерческой кухне. Оба бренда предлагают 2.0 мм варианты, но коммерческие кухни требуют 3.0 мм минимум.

IL-канал — оба бренда нуждаются в верификации

Оба Altro и Polyflor достигают IL через спец-импортёров — у обоих нет Sika-style exclusive distributor at scale. Для project тендеров — phone-верифицируй с местным импортёром:

  • Наличие на складе конкретного SKU и цвета
  • Lead time (обычно 4–8 недель для non-stocked вариантов)
  • Approved IL-applicator pool — установка safety vinyl technique-heavy, heat-welded швы требуют trained installers
  • Warranty registration процесс

IL-applicator pool значительно перекрывается между двумя брендами — большинство installers handle оба. Выбор бренда therefore upstream install team, не downstream.

Финальный вывод

Altro — правильная спецификация когда lifetime warranty language, NHS-style brand recognition или Whiterock wall integration имеют значение. Polyflor — правильная спецификация когда range depth, wood-effect decorative, heavy-duty 3.5 мм или 5–15% cost-savings драйвят решение. Для большинства IL-проектов бренды взаимозаменяемы в функциональной spec — выбирай по дифференциации, которую твой проект ценит, и по IL-импортёру, с которым у твоей команды самые сильные отношения.
