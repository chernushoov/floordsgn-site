Sustainable Flooring — Cradle-to-Cradle + EPD

May 2026 · 7 min read

Sustainability claims on floor systems have moved from marketing language to measurable specification. Three frameworks dominate the IL tender stack: Cradle-to-Cradle Products Innovation Institute certification (C2C, levels Bronze through Platinum), EPD (Environmental Product Declaration per ISO 14025), and embodied-carbon calculations (CO2e per m² of installed floor across the full lifecycle). Specification-grade sustainability now requires at least one of these three with named numerical values; "eco-friendly" without backing is reject-on-sight at tender stage. This page walks the three frameworks, names the IL-available systems with current certification, and ends with the tender language that ensures sustainability claims hold up at audit.

Framework 1 of 3 — C2C

Cradle-to-Cradle Products Innovation Institute

The C2C Products Innovation Institute certifies products on five categories with bronze / silver / gold / platinum scoring per category. Lowest category score sets overall certification level (a product cannot reach platinum overall without platinum in all five categories).

CategoryWhat it measures
Material HealthChemistry of constituent materials — no banned substances; preference for restorative substances
Material ReutilisationPathway for end-of-life recovery — recycling rate, reverse logistics, design for disassembly
Renewable EnergyManufacturing energy from renewable sources — site-level + supply-chain renewable percentage
Water StewardshipWater consumption + discharge management at manufacturing site + supply chain
Social FairnessLabour practices at manufacturing + supply chain — fair wages, working conditions, community impact

For tender-grade IL projects: C2C Silver+ is the practical threshold. Gold + Platinum products are rare in floor category and command 20-50% premium over conventional alternatives.

Framework 2 of 3 — EPD

EPD (Environmental Product Declaration)

ISO 14025 standardised document declaring the environmental impact of a product across its lifecycle. Verified by independent third-party. Covers product Material extraction → Manufacturing → Distribution → Installation → Use → End-of-Life. Each phase quantified with named impact categories.

Impact categoryUnitWhat it measures
Global Warming Potential (GWP)kg CO2e / m²Total greenhouse-gas emissions from product lifecycle
Ozone Depletion (ODP)kg CFC-11e / m²Substances depleting stratospheric ozone
Acidification (AP)kg SO2e / m²Substances acidifying soil + water
Eutrophication (EP)kg PO4e / m²Nutrient runoff to water
Primary Energy DemandMJ / m²Total energy consumption
Water Usem³ / m²Net water consumption

EPD format follows EN 15804 for construction products. Floor-system EPDs typically cover all stages from raw materials through end-of-life recycling/disposal scenarios.

Framework 3 of 3 — Embodied carbon

Embodied Carbon (kg CO2e per m²)

Embodied carbon focuses on the GWP component of the EPD — total CO2-equivalent emissions per square metre of installed floor. Used directly for project carbon budgets, BREEAM/LEED credit calculation, and IL Green Building Standard ת״י 5281 compliance.

System typeTypical kg CO2e / m²Notes
Polished concrete3-8Lowest embodied carbon — substrate IS the floor, minimal additional material
Microtopping 2-3 mm15-25Thin cementitious layer + PU topcoat
Marmoleum (linoleum)8-15Bio-based binder + jute backing; carbon-negative manufacturing claim from Forbo
Cement terrazzo20-35Heritage-grade; thicker pour but cement-based
Epoxy SL 2-4 mm25-45Petrochemical resin + curing
PU-cement 6-9 mm40-65Polyurethane + cement; thicker pour higher embodied carbon
Vinyl tile / sheet30-50PVC + plasticisers
Porcelain tile + adhesive35-55Kiln-fired ceramic + cementitious adhesive

For IL Green Building Standard ת״י 5281: lower embodied carbon improves credit score. Polished concrete + Marmoleum dominate the low-embodied-carbon category; PU-cement + tile sit at the higher end. The trade-off: low-embodied-carbon systems often have shorter lifecycle, so per-year lifecycle CO2e may not match the initial embodied carbon difference.

  • Sika Sikafloor PurCem: Cradle-to-Cradle Bronze; EPD published per EN 15804. Industrial sustainability leader.

Tender language for sustainability

Direct paste into BOQ for projects with sustainability requirements (BREEAM / LEED / ת״י 5281 / corporate ESG).

"Floor system shall demonstrate sustainability via at least one of the following backed certifications: Cradle-to-Cradle Bronze minimum (Silver+ preferred) from Cradle-to-Cradle Products Innovation Institute; OR EPD per ISO 14025 + EN 15804 from independent third-party verifier with quantified GWP / ODP / AP / EP impact values; OR embodied carbon ≤ [target] kg CO2e per m² per project carbon budget calculation. Certification documents attached to handover. Marketing claims of 'sustainable' or 'eco-friendly' without backed certification rejected at submission stage."

Common sustainability-spec mistakes

  • "Eco-friendly" claim without backing. Marketing language. Reject submissions without C2C / EPD / quantified embodied-carbon documentation.
  • EPD older than 5 years. EPDs expire (typical validity 5 years from issue). Verify current validity at tender stage.
  • C2C certification at level lower than tender requirement. A product C2C Bronze cannot substitute for tender-required C2C Silver. Verify level explicitly.
  • Embodied carbon ignoring installation + use-phase + end-of-life. A1-A3 cradle-to-gate is incomplete; specify A1-A4 cradle-to-grave for full lifecycle accounting.
  • Substitution post-award without re-verification. If applicator substitutes a different product post-award, sustainability claim must be re-verified for new product.
  • VOC compliance confused with sustainability. EMICODE EC1 (low VOC) is indoor air quality, not sustainability. Both may be required but they're separate.

Lifecycle thinking — beyond installation

Sustainability for floors extends beyond installation:

  • Use phase: Cleaning chemistry water + energy use over 20-year lifecycle can exceed installation impact. Maintenance protocol matters.
  • Lifecycle length: A 30-year floor at higher initial embodied carbon may have lower per-year impact than a 15-year floor at lower initial embodied carbon.
  • End-of-life: Recyclable / re-densifiable floors (polished concrete, vinyl, Marmoleum) score higher than resin systems that go to landfill at end-of-life.
  • Substrate reuse: Polished concrete reuses the existing slab, eliminating new material from substrate to surface.
  • Repair vs replace: Repairable floors (tile, polished concrete) win over non-repairable (resin) at year 15+ when partial damage occurs.

IL Green Building Standard ת״י 5281 — flooring credit

The IL Green Building Standard (Israeli equivalent of LEED / BREEAM) awards points for:

  • Use of certified-sustainable floor products (1-3 points).
  • Low embodied carbon (proportional to credit calculation).
  • EMICODE EC1+ low VOC (1-2 points).
  • Recyclable / re-densifiable end-of-life pathway.
  • Local manufacturer / supplier (where applicable).

For ת״י 5281 Gold / Platinum buildings, certified-sustainable floor specification is essentially required, not optional.

Cost considerations

Certified-sustainable floor adds 10-40% to baseline cost depending on certification level + system:

  • C2C Bronze: +5-10% material premium typically.
  • C2C Silver: +15-25% material premium.
  • C2C Gold / Platinum: +30-50% material premium (where available).
  • EPD-published product: +0-5% (most major manufacturers publish for major lines).
  • Embodied-carbon calculation: ₪3,000-8,000 per project (third-party calculation).

Final read

Sustainability for floor systems is now specifiable, measurable, and auditable. C2C + EPD + embodied carbon form the IL tender stack; "eco-friendly" without backing has no value. Specify the certification level required; require the documentation attached to handover; refuse substitutions without re-verification. Sustainability is not free, but the cost is predictable — and for ת״י 5281 Gold / Platinum projects it's a tender pre-requisite, not a nice-to-have.
