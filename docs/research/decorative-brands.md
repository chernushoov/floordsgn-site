## 1. Topciment (Spain)

- **HQ / origin:** Valencia, Spain (manufactured in Manises). Founded as the Spanish microcement pioneer.
- **Distribution:** Dealer network in 58+ countries (per their own materials). Global. Israel **[verify]** — no explicit IL dealer found on their public dealer page; product is sold via independent applicators.
- **Product lines:**
  - **Sttandard** — traditional two-component (A+B liquid polymer + powder) microcement. Sub-products: Sttandard Microbase, Sttandard Microdeck (floors), Sttandard Microfino (walls — silky), Sttandard Microstone (exteriors).
  - **Evoluttion** — one-component system, factory-pigmented, ready to mix. Easier for less experienced installers.
  - **Efectto / Efectto Quartz** — newest line, ready-to-use, 4 granulometries.
  - **Atlanttic** — for swimming pools and constant-water-contact surfaces, non-slip pool-edge variant.
  - **Industtrial** — epoxy-based industrial microcement.
- **Layer system (typical):** primer (with fibreglass mesh on critical joints) → 2 coats Microbase → 2 coats Microfino/Microdeck → 2 coats Presealer (water-based PU) → 2 coats Topsealer (PU final).
- **Use cases:** residential floors and walls, commercial retail, hospitality, swimming pools (Atlanttic), industrial floors (Industtrial). Indoor + outdoor.
- **Unique:** widest color/finish catalog; in-house pigment system; dedicated training academy with face-to-face + on-site + online + video modules; 4,000 m² factory + R&D lab in Manises.
- **Installer pros:** rich documentation, technical data sheets per product, broad accessory ecosystem (mesh, primers, sealers from same brand), strong color predictability.
- **Installer cons:** 2K systems require dosing discipline; multi-layer system means longer downtime than 1K ready-to-use brands; price tier higher than commodity Chinese imports.
- **Sika analog:** **SikaDecor-801 Nature** (polymer-enriched cementitious microcement, similar layer architecture).

## 4. Keim (Germany)

- **HQ / origin:** Keimfarben GmbH, Diedorf (near Augsburg) + Alteno (near Berlin), Germany. Founded 1878 by Adolf Wilhelm Keim — inventor of mineral silicate paint.
- **Distribution:** Worldwide. Subsidiary Keim Mineral Coatings of America for the US. Strong EU presence. Israel **[verify]**.
- **Product lines (selected):**
  - **Granital** — silicate emulsion paint (since 1962), classic facade.
  - **Soldalit** — sol-silicate paint (2002), universal substrates.
  - **Purkristalat** — pure 2-component silicate, heritage spec.
  - **Veramin** — mineral paint for any building.
  - **Lignosil** — mineral composite coating for wood (since 2013).
  - **Concrete repair + surface protection systems** — facade restoration.
  - **Renders and fillers, natural stone repair systems, thermal insulation composite systems.**
- **Technology — "silicification":** liquid potassium silicate (waterglass) binder + mineral fillers + inorganic pigments. The binder chemically reacts with the mineral substrate (concrete, render, natural stone) to form an insoluble bond, not just a surface film. Result: extreme weather resistance, breathability, mineral aesthetic.
- **Use cases:** primarily **walls and facades, not flooring**. Heritage restoration, mineral facades, breathable interior walls. **Do not position Keim as a floor brand in customer content** — it sits next to Marmorino/Tadelakt on the wall side of the conversation.
- **Unique:** 140+ years; original inventor of the category; light-stable inorganic pigments only; bonded mineral chemistry rather than film-forming.
- **Installer pros:** unmatched durability on mineral substrates; reference standard for heritage architects.
- **Installer cons:** technique-sensitive, only bonds to mineral substrates (not over plastic/synthetic primers); price-premium.
- **Sika analog:** **Sikagard mineral coatings** for facade / concrete protection **[verify]** — Sika has no direct silicate-paint inventor heritage.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.keim.com/company/
  - https://www.keim.com/silicate-paint/
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keimfarben

## 6. Senso (Netherlands)

- **HQ / origin:** Netherlands. Poured in NL, installed worldwide.
- **Distribution:** Direct + applicator network across EU, UK, US, growing global. Israel **[verify]**.
- **Product lines:**

## 11. Tadelakt (Moroccan traditional)

- **Status:** **Traditional Moroccan technique**, originated in Marrakech region. Not a brand — applicators source raw lime + soap and follow the technique. Some brand-packaged Tadelakt kits exist (Stucco Italiano, Tadelakt US, Earthaus).
- **What it is:** waterproof lime plaster for hammams, baths, palace walls, riad residences. Etymology from Arabic "tadlīk" = "to rub / massage".
- **Technology:** lime plaster is rammed and polished with a river stone, then treated with **soft soap → saponification reaction → calcium soaps (insoluble in water) form a waterproof skin**. Chemistry, not coating.
- **Layer thickness:** **[verify]** typically 3–8 mm system in 2–3 coats.
- **Use cases:** bathrooms, hammams, sinks, water vessels, pools, exterior + interior walls + ceilings + roofs + floors.
- **Unique:** **chemically waterproof through saponification, not a sealer**; entirely natural, VOC-free, mold/mildew resistant naturally; no seams or grout.
- **Installer pros:** unique design narrative (Marrakech heritage); naturally antibacterial.
- **Installer cons:** technique-heavy — true Tadelakt takes days of hand-polishing; installer skill is rare in Israel; not warranty-backed in the industrial sense.
- **Sika analog:** None — heritage technique outside Sika's category.
- **Sources:**

  - https://earthausplaster.com/pages/tadelakt-interior-finish

## 12. Marmorino (Venetian — Italian traditional)

- **Status:** **Italian traditional finish**, originally Venetian; multiple brands produce Marmorino (Meoded, Stucco Italiano, Romabio, Tintoretto, Decora). Not one brand.
- **Composition:** lime putty (slaked lime) + crushed marble dust; pigment added on-site or in factory.
- **Technology:** layer-built lime plaster, troweled, can be matte/satin/polished depending on finish technique. Highly breathable, antibacterial, naturally fungistatic. Suitable interior + exterior.
- **Layer thickness:** 1–3 mm.
- **Use cases:** premium walls (not floors typically), feature walls, fireplaces, retail interiors. Resembles polished marble or limestone depending on finish.
- **Unique:** centuries-old Venetian heritage; breathable mineral chemistry; on-site finishing controls aesthetic.
- **Installer pros:** premium positioning, design-press friendly, breathable so safe over old masonry.
- **Installer cons:** wall application only — not for floor abrasion; technique-heavy.
- **Sika analog:** Sika decorative wall coatings **[verify]** — but Marmorino is craft, not industrial spec.
