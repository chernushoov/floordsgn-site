# Flooring Industry — Portals, Magazines, Standards & Sources

Curated reference for FloorDSGN: industry portals, trade magazines, technical resources, and content sources where decorative AND industrial flooring news/updates/tutorials/standards are published.

Purpose:
1. **External monitoring list** — pulse-check the industry for trends, new products, technical updates.
2. **Outbound citation pool** — authoritative sources to cite on FloorDSGN site (improves SEO trust + outbound link diversity + signals topical authority to Google).
3. **Resources page seed** — content for a public-facing "Industry Resources" page on the site.

Categories: (1) Trade magazines, (2) Trade associations, (3) Standards bodies + technical docs, (4) YouTube install channels, (5) Architecture/design portals.

## 2. Trade Associations

### Concrete + Resin Flooring

- **ICRI — International Concrete Repair Institute** — https://www.icri.org — The authoritative body for concrete repair, surface preparation, CSP profiles. Owner of CSP 1-10 system. Mandatory citation when discussing surface prep.
- **FeRFA — UK Resin Flooring Association** — https://ferfa.org.uk — UK authority for epoxy/PU/MMA resin floors. Publishes the FeRFA Type 1-8 classification (the European standard taxonomy for resin floor systems). Free downloadable guides.
- **EFNARC — European Federation of National Associations Representing producers/applicators of specialist building products for Concrete** — https://www.efnarc.org — EU-wide technical guidelines (industrial floors, repair mortars).
- **ACI — American Concrete Institute** — https://www.concrete.org — Owns ACI 302.1R (Guide to Concrete Floor and Slab Construction) and ACI 360 (Slabs-on-Ground). Cite for all flatwork-design discussions.
- **ASCC — American Society of Concrete Contractors** — https://www.ascconline.org — Position statements on flatwork, finishing, cracking. Practical-contractor angle.
- **NFCA — National Flooring Contractors Association (US)** — https://www.nationalfloorcontractors.com — Member directory, training.
- **CFA — Concrete Foundations Association** — https://cfawalls.org — Cast-in-place concrete focus, adjacent to slab work.
- **TCNA — Tile Council of North America** — https://www.tcnatile.com — Adjacent to floor industry; movement-joint and waterproofing standards used in tile-over-resin assemblies.
- **WFCA — World Floor Covering Association** — https://wfca.org — US trade body for floor-covering retailers/installers.
- **PDCA — Painting and Decorating Contractors of America** — https://www.pdca.org — Adjacent; covers floor coatings via coating-applicator trade.
- **SSPC / AMPP — Association for Materials Protection and Performance** — https://www.ampp.org — Industrial coatings standards (SSPC-SP 13 for concrete surface prep is referenced alongside ICRI 310.2R).

- **FEICA — Association of the European Adhesive & Sealant Industry** — https://www.feica.eu — Adhesive standards used in flooring assemblies.
- **DBV — German Concrete Association (Deutscher Beton- und Bautechnik-Verein)** — https://www.betonverein.de — German technical authority on concrete; publishes DBV merkblätter (technical leaflets) used on industrial floor projects.

## 3. Standards Bodies + Technical Documents

### European Standards (EN)

- **EN 13813 — Screed material and floor screeds — Screed materials. Properties and requirements** — https://www.dinmedia.de/en/standard/din-en-13813/55603870 — The European master standard for floor screeds (CT/CA/MA/SR/AS classification). Resin screeds = SR. Mandatory citation in any EU-spec floor.
- **EN 1504 (parts 1–10) — Products and systems for the protection and repair of concrete structures** — https://standards.cencenelec.eu/ — Ten-part EU standard covering products (1504-2 surface protection, 1504-3 structural repair, 1504-9 general principles, etc.). Cite for all repair-mortar / coating work.
- **EN 1504-2** specifically covers surface-protection systems for concrete (the part most relevant to floor coatings).
- **EN 13670 — Execution of concrete structures** — https://standards.cencenelec.eu — Construction-side EU standard.
- **EN 206 — Concrete: specification, performance, production and conformity** — https://standards.cencenelec.eu — Master EU standard for concrete itself.
- **EN 14411 — Ceramic tiles** — https://standards.cencenelec.eu — Adjacent for tile-over-screed.
- **EN ISO 13473 — Surface texture / macrotexture** — slip-related, used alongside DIN 51130.
- **EN 14904 — Sports floors** — relevant for sports-resin floors.
- Buy/preview: **CEN** https://standards.cencenelec.eu, **DIN Media** https://www.dinmedia.de, **BSI** https://www.bsigroup.com (UK), **UNI** https://www.uni.com (Italy), **AENOR** https://www.aenor.com (Spain).

### German DIN Standards

- **DIN 51130 — Testing of floor coverings — Anti-slip property — Walking method, ramp test (R-class)** — https://www.dinmedia.de/en/standard/din-51130/196898059 — The R9-R13 slip-resistance classification used across EU industrial spec. Mandatory citation on every wet-area or commercial floor page.
- **DIN 51097 — Anti-slip for barefoot areas (A/B/C classification)** — https://www.dinmedia.de/en/standard/din-51097 — For pool decks, showers, locker rooms.
- **DIN 18560 — Screeds in building construction** — https://www.dinmedia.de/en/standard/din-18560 — Includes the CM (Calcium Carbide) moisture-test method; the European counterpart/alternative to ASTM F2170.
- **DIN 18365 — Floor covering work** (VOB Part C) — German contract-execution standard for floor coverings.
- **DIN 18202 — Tolerances in building construction (flatness)** — referenced in every German screed/floor spec.

### ASTM (American) Standards

- **ASTM C881 — Standard Specification for Epoxy-Resin-Base Bonding Systems for Concrete** — https://store.astm.org/c0881_c0881m-15.html — Cite for any epoxy primer/bonding-agent discussion.
- **ASTM F2170 — Standard Test Method for Determining Relative Humidity in Concrete Floor Slabs Using In Situ Probes** — https://store.astm.org/f2170-23a.html — The RH-probe standard. 75–85% RH thresholds for resin floor go/no-go decisions. Companion: ASTM F1869 (anhydrous CaCl moisture, older method).
- **ASTM F710 — Standard Practice for Preparing Concrete Floors to Receive Resilient Flooring** — https://store.astm.org/f0710-21.html — Substrate prep practice.
- **ASTM C779 — Abrasion resistance of horizontal concrete surfaces** — https://store.astm.org/c0779_c0779m-19.html
- **ASTM C309 — Liquid Membrane-Forming Compounds for Curing Concrete** — https://store.astm.org/c0309-19.html
- **ASTM E1155 — Floor flatness FF / floor levelness FL** — https://store.astm.org/e1155-20.html — F-numbers; critical for warehouse/distribution-center spec.
- **ASTM C39 — Compressive strength** — https://store.astm.org/c0039_c0039m-23.html
- **ASTM C928 — Packaged, Dry, Rapid-Hardening Cementitious Materials for Concrete Repairs** — https://store.astm.org/c0928_c0928m-20a.html
- **ASTM D7234 — Pull-off Adhesion Strength of Coatings on Concrete** — https://store.astm.org/d7234-21.html — Cite when discussing coating adhesion.

### ICRI Guidelines

- **ICRI 310.2R-2013 — Selecting and Specifying Concrete Surface Preparation for Sealers, Coatings, Polymer Overlays, and Concrete Repair** — https://store.icri.org/item/3102r2013-english-pdf-selecting-concrete-surface-preparation-sealers-coatings-polymer-overlays-concrete-repair-342521 — Owns the CSP 1-10 visual scale (rubber comparator chips). - **ICRI 330.1 — Guide for Concrete Repair Material Selection** — https://store.icri.org

### Other International

## Usage / Workflow

### For SEO citation strategy
1. **Always cite EN 13813, DIN 51130, ICRI 310.2R, ASTM F2170, ת״י 466/1923** on relevant product/material pages — these are the most-searched standards in our niche.
2. **Outbound link diversity**: rotate 3-5 authoritative outbound links per long-form article. Don't link to one source 50 times.
