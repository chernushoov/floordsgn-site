# Industrial & Commercial Seamless Flooring — Brand Inventory

Research brief for FloorDSGN (Israel) — competitive landscape vs. Sikafloor, with technical depth for comparison content.

- **Compiled**: 2026-05-27
- **Scope**: industrial epoxy, PU, PU-cement (PUMA/MMA), decorative resin, commercial vinyl/safety, sports/rubber.
- **Marker** `[verify]` = claim is plausible from public material but needs primary-source check before publication.
- **Currency**: technical numbers are typical/representative — always confirm against current TDS at time of spec.

## 0. Reference frame — what we compare

### 0.1 European standards (binding in EU + adopted in IL via SI)

| Standard | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **EN 13813** | Screeds and materials for screeds — properties & requirements. Defines reaction-to-fire (Bfl-s1 etc.), wear (AR-class), bond strength (B-class), compressive strength (C-class), flexural (F-class), impact (IR-class) ([resdev guide](https://www.resdev.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/european-standards-for-flooring-products.pdf)) |
| **EN 1504-2** | Surface protection products for concrete — relevant for resin coatings used as concrete protection (CE-marked alternative path to EN 13813) |
| **EN 13501-1** | Reaction to fire — flooring usually targets `Bfl-s1` (low smoke, low flame spread) |
| **EN 13036-4** | Pendulum test for slip resistance (PTV) |
| **EN 16165** | Replaced DIN 51130/51097 in 2021 — still uses R9–R13 ramp classes ([slip-not.co.uk](https://www.slip-not.co.uk/pages/slip-resistance-ratings-r9-r13)) |
| **EN 1081 / EN 61340-5-1** | Electrical resistance, ESD floors |
| **EN 13845** | Resilient floor coverings — vinyl with anti-slip particles |

### 0.2 German DIN R-class (still the de-facto industrial spec)

| R-class | Slope | Use case |
|---|---|---|
| R9 | 6°–10° | Dry indoor, entrances, retail, offices |
| R10 | 10°–19° | Light wet — public WC, garages |
| R11 | 19°–27° | Commercial kitchens, bathrooms, light food |
| R12 | 27°–35° | Industrial kitchens, dairies, bottling |
| R13 | >35° | Abattoirs, fish processing, heavy wet industrial |

Source: [Slip-Not R-class guide](https://www.slip-not.co.uk/pages/slip-resistance-ratings-r9-r13), [Safety Direct America DIN 51130](https://safetydirectamerica.com/germanys-din-51130-slip-test-whats-it-good-for/).

### 0.3 DIN 18560 — industrial screeds

`DIN 18560-7` specifies heavy-duty industrial floor screeds (cementitious, mastic asphalt, synthetic resin). Defines AR-class (BCA wear) for industrial use. Often referenced together with EN 13813.

### 0.4 ASTM (USA-side spec, useful when comparing Stonhard/SW/Sika-US data)

- **ASTM C881** — Standard spec for epoxy-resin-base bonding systems for concrete
- **ASTM C579** — Compressive strength of chemical-resistant mortars/grouts/monolithic surfacings
- **ASTM D4541** — Pull-off adhesion of coatings
- **ASTM F1869 / F2170** — Moisture (CaCl₂ + in-situ RH) — gate test before any resin install

### 0.5 Israeli standards (ת״י)

- **ת״י 466 (SI 466)** — Concrete code (parts 1–5) — design/execution of reinforced & prestressed concrete; the *substrate* code, not the resin coat code. Resin systems above it follow EN 13813 / EN 1504-2 by reference. ([SII catalog](https://www.sii.org.il/he/lobby/standardization/standard-page/?id=8da01f10-839e-48c7-8101-07143787bba0_HE))
- **ת״י 1918** — Accessibility (slip resistance for accessible routes) — relevant for public/commercial spec.
- **ת״י 5566** — Adhesives for ceramic tiles (Eurocol/Mapei tile-grade products fall here).
- `[verify]` SII does not publish a dedicated resin-floor code analogous to EN 13813 — IL spec writers cite EN 13813 directly.

### 0.6 FeRFA Types 1–8 (UK Resin Flooring Association — the *operational* taxonomy)

| Type | Thickness | Description | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | <150 µm | Floor seal (2+ coats, solvent/water-borne) | Dust-proofing, light retail |
| 2 | 150–300 µm | Floor coating | Light traffic warehouse |
| 3 | 300–1000 µm | High-build coating | Medium-duty warehouse, garages |
| 4 | 2–3 mm | Multi-layer (flow + broadcast) | Workshops, light food |
| 5 | 2–3 mm | Flow-applied (self-smoothing) | Pharma, electronics, decorative |
| 6 | 4–6 mm | Resin screed (trowel, lightly filled) | Industrial kitchens, breweries |
| 7 | 4–6 mm | Heavy-duty flowable | Process areas with chemical spillage |
| 8 | 6–9 mm | Heavy-duty trowel-applied | Heavy engineering, abattoirs, chemical plants |

Source: [FeRFA Standard Classification, Oct 2024](https://ferfa.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/The-FeRFA-Standard-Classification-Types-October-2024.pdf), [ferfa.org.uk/standard](https://ferfa.org.uk/standard/).

### 0.7 ICRI Concrete Surface Profile (CSP 1–10)

Before any resin: the substrate must be prepped to a specific CSP. ICRI publishes CSP rubber comparators 1–10:
- CSP 1–3: acid etch / light grinding — only for thin coatings (Type 1–2)
- CSP 3–5: shot blast / heavy grind — high-build (Type 3) and self-leveling (Type 5)
- CSP 5–9: scarify / scabble — mortars (Type 6–8)
- CSP 10 (added 2013): aggressive — concrete repair only

Reference: [ICRI 310.2R-2013](https://www.icri.org/resources/icri-technical-publications/), [DeFelsko CSP chips](https://www.defelsko.com/csp).

## 1. Sika / Sikafloor — the benchmark we compete with

- **HQ**: Baar, Switzerland.
- **IL distributor**: **Gilar Ltd** (גילאר) — exclusive Sika representative in Israel since long-standing. [gilar.co.il](https://gilar.co.il/) — catalog covers Sikafloor PU, epoxy, PurCem, PMMA, Sikadur repair, ([Sika ME distributors](https://industry.sika.com/en/home/advanced-resins/distribution/distribution-middle-east.html)).
- **Position**: world #1 by revenue in resinous flooring. Full chemistry coverage (epoxy / PU / PMMA / PU-cement). Strongest channel + tech-service network globally.
- **Flagship industrial lines**:
  - **Sikafloor PurCem** (PU-cement): HM-20, HB-22, 20/29/31 PurCem LP. 6–9 mm. R-class up to R13 with broadcast. Food & beverage HACCP-certified, thermal-shock resistant (steam clean at 9 mm). ([HM-20 TDS](https://gcc.sika.com/en/construction/flooring-and-coating/industrial-flooring/resin-cementitioussystem/sikafloor-purcemhm-20.html))
  - **Sikafloor MultiDur** — epoxy heavy-duty self-leveling and mortar (FeRFA Type 5–7). [usa.sika.com MultiDur](https://usa.sika.com/en/construction/floor-wall/flooring-systems/sikafloor-multidur.html)
  - **Sikafloor MultiFlex** — PU elastic crack-bridging / parking decks.
  - **Sikafloor PMMA** (MMA) — fast-cure (1–2 h), cold-store, repair shutdown windows.
- **Where Sika is strong**: tech-service depth, warranty chain, single-source from primer→topcoat→sealant→repair (Sikadur), specifier reputation, IL channel (Gilar).
- **Where Sika is weak (attack vectors)**: price premium 20–40% over Mapei/Master Builders at equivalent spec `[verify locally]`; channel through Gilar means longer lead times for non-stocked SKUs vs. local manufacturers; PurCem aesthetic options narrower than Flowcrete Flowfresh decorative range.

## 20. Strategic notes — how to compete content-wise vs. Sika in IL

1. **Sika owns the spec — but Gilar is the bottleneck**. Lead times, MOQ, support depth all flow through Gilar. A *local* contractor with multi-brand stock (Sika+Mapei+MBS+Flowcrete) can beat Sika-only on availability + flexibility.
2. **Flowfresh's Polygiene antimicrobial** is the single most distinctive technical claim no Sikafloor SKU matches at parity — comparison content should center this for food/pharma prospects.
3. **Ucrete (BASF/MBS)** is the *most respected* PU-cement among engineers — write a comparison piece "Sikafloor PurCem vs Ucrete vs Flowfresh — what an engineer should check" — it's the most-searched buyer-stage query.
4. **Stonhard single-source warranty** — Sika cannot match this in IL (Sika sells material; install by third-party contractor under contractor warranty). Worth flagging in customer education content.
5. **MMA fast-track** (Sika Sikafloor PMMA vs MasterTop 1817PC vs Triflex vs Kemperol) — only category where Sika is *not* the default — these German specialists outclass on MMA chemistry and shutdown-window installs.
6. **Standards literacy is a moat**. IL spec writers cite EN 13813 + DIN 51130 + ת״י 466 — content that *teaches* the spec (FeRFA Type, R-class, CSP, AR-class) earns specifier trust. Sika's own docs assume you already know — gap to fill.
7. **Ardex below + Sika on top** is a common real-world spec — content should acknowledge this rather than positioning brands as mutually exclusive.
