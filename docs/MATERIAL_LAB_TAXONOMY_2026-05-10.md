# Material Lab — Industry Taxonomy (research output 2026-05-10)

8 domain agents researched real polymer-coatings industry standards. Findings below feed directly into `materials.config.json` v7.

## R1 — Terrazzo

**Aggregates** (16 real types):
Carrara 0-4mm white marble; Bianco Carrara 4-8mm; Rosso Verona 8-12mm warm pink; Nero Marquina 8-12mm black w/white veins; Travertino Romano 4-12mm beige; Giallo Siena 8-12mm yellow; Basalt 4-8mm anthracite; Granite Sardo 8-12mm grey; Quartz 0-4mm milky; Recycled glass 4-12mm (colored cullet); Mirror chip 4-8mm silver; Mother-of-pearl 2-6mm iridescent; Brass dust 0-2mm; Copper flake 2-6mm; Palladiana 25-40mm (cement only); Coin embed (handlaid).

**Pairing rules:** 0-4mm + 4-8mm → epoxy only (Sikafloor EM-10). 8-12mm → epoxy or cement. Palladiana 12-40mm → cement only (Sikafloor CS-31), min thickness 30mm. Brass/copper accents require epoxy (cement oxidizes metals). Mirror/glass → epoxy only.

**Dividers (саргели — Hebrew סרגל "ruler"):** Brass 3/4/6mm, Aluminum 4mm, Zinc 3mm, Stainless 3mm, PVC T-profile, Black bakelite 4mm, Custom 3D-printed.

**Binder:** Sikafloor EM-10 epoxy (8-15mm); Sikafloor CS-31 cementitious (30-50mm); polyaspartic (UV-stable, fast); acrylic-modified cement (thin overlay 6-10mm).

**Finish:** matte 50-100 grit; satin 200; honed 400; polished 800; high-polish 1500; mirror 3000+ with Li densifier; carnauba wax (heritage); PU sealer Sikafloor 305W.

**Accents:** mica gold/pearl, metallic pigment Sika ColorFlake, photoluminescent GlowStone, recycled ceramic chips, CNC brass logo inlay.

## R2 — Epoxy SL (industrial) + decorative

**Industrial SL base:** Sikafloor-263 SL (2-4mm); BASF MasterTop 1273; Mapei Mapefloor I 500 W.

**Broadcast quartz:** Sika ColorQuartz / Quarzolith / Bayferrox-coated. Sizes: 0.4-0.7mm (smooth-tech R10), 0.7-1.2mm (warehouse R11), 1.2-2.4mm (ramps/wet R12-R13). Density: light 2-3 kg/m² / full broadcast 5-7 kg/m² (saturated, refusal).

**Industrial markings:** RAL 1023 yellow (forklift safety, 100mm), RAL 6024 green (pedestrian, 150mm), RAL 3020 red (fire lane, 150mm), RAL 9016 white (parking bay, 50mm), yellow/black 45° hatched no-go (100mm).

**Industrial dividers:** brass 3mm (architectural joints), yellow-anodized aluminum 5mm (forklift lanes), black PVC compensation joint (every 6×6m SL grid).

**Additives:** Sikafloor-235 ESD (carbon black, 10⁶-10⁹ Ω); Sikafloor-262 AS (copper grid + carbon, conductive); Mapefloor Bioblock (silver-ion antimicrobial); Jessup Glo Brite phosphor; aluminum oxide 60/80 mesh anti-slip grit.

**Decorative:** Sikafloor DecoDur EB-Flake (vinyl chip); DecoDur Metallic FX; Mapei Ultratop Loft; Flowcrete Mondeco.

**Vinyl flakes (Torginol, Decoflake, Polymer Crystals):**
Sizes: 1/16" small speckle (retail); 1/8" standard residential; 1/4" showroom/garage; 1/2" loft accent.
Blends: solid; 2-color (Torginol "Domino"); 3-color custom (Torginol "Granite").
Coverage: light 40-60%; medium 70-85%; full broadcast ≥95% (refusal).

**Mica/pearl:** Merck Iriodin 100 pearl; Iriodin 504 copper; Iriodin 300 gold. Dose 50-150 g/m² in base.

**Metallic FX pigments:** Eckart Stapa Metallux silver/copper/bronze/graphite; technique = 2-layer pour + solvent veil.

## R3 — Polished concrete (CPC standard)

**Aggregate exposure (CPC Concrete Polishing Council classes):**
- Class A — cream, no exposure, depth 0mm
- Class B — salt-and-pepper, fine aggregate, depth 1.5mm
- Class C — medium aggregate, depth 3mm
- Class D — large/full aggregate, depth 5+mm (terrazzo-like)

**Polish levels (CPC gloss / DOI per ASTM E430):**
- L1 — ground matte, 200/400 grit, satin matte, DOI <10 (warehouse)
- L2 — honed satin, 800/1500 grit, semi-polish, DOI 40-69 (retail/office)
- L3 — polished gloss, 1500/3000 grit, high-polish, DOI 70-79 (showroom/lobby)
- L4 — highly polished, 3000/8000 grit, mirror, DOI 80+ (premium hospitality)

**Densifier:** Lithium silicate recommended (Sikafloor ProSeal W, Ashford Formula) — high dust resist, low whitening. Sodium/potassium silicate (older), colloidal silica. Apply after 400-grit, before 800-grit.

**Aggregate types:** natural stone (granite/basalt/quartz/marble); recycled glass terrazzo-look; metallic dispersed (Sikafloor MetalTop, Mastertop 100); color hardener dry-shake; seeded aggregate broadcast.

**Stain/dye:** acid stain (mottled brown/green/blue, reactive, post-grind pre-densifier); water dye (full saturated, between 800 and 1500 grit); reactive stain (Smith Color Floor).

**Sealers:** lithium guard (Sika ProSeal W natural sheen); PU guard (oleophobic+hydrophobic satin/gloss); stain-repel (Prosoco LSGuard, Ameripolish 3DHS).

## R4 — PU-cement

**Sikafloor PurCem family (real SKU mapping):**
- 21 PurCem (smooth slurry) → 4-6mm light-duty, foot traffic, dry packaging, -25 to +70°C
- 21N PurCem (satin/textured) → 6-9mm medium-duty, wet kitchens/dairies/bottling, -40 to +90°C
- 22N PurCem (HD trowel) → 9-12mm heavy-duty, meat/fish/breweries/CIP/freezer, -40 to +120°C, thermal-shock CIP/steam-clean
- MT-19 PurCem (cove-base trowel-grade)

**Competitors:** BASF Ucrete UD200/MF/FL; Flowcrete Flowfresh; Acrylicon AC100/400; Resyn Resdek.

**Broadcast media:**
- Silicon carbide 0.5-1.2mm → R12-R13 (wet kitchens, fish/meat, ramps)
- Aluminum oxide 0.6-1.5mm → R10-R12 (general food, balanced clean/grip)
- Color quartz 0.4-0.8mm → R10-R11 (dry packaging, decorative)
- Ceramic granule 0.7-1.4mm → R11-R12 (FDA/NSF zones, non-staining)

Distribution: light (R10), medium (R11-R12), heavy/saturated (R12-R13).

**Cove-base radii:** 50/75/100/150mm. Hot-coved (wet-on-wet during pour with PurCem 21N/22N) vs cold-applied (Sikafloor MT-19 PurCem trowel-grade post-cure). Default food: R75 hot-coved; R100-R150 for high-pressure washdown.

**Drain types:** linear slot (slope 1:80, kitchens/brewery); radial gully (1:50, meat/poultry); perimeter channel (1:80, dairy/bottling); point drain (1:50, small prep zones).

**Food RAL palette:**
- 7037 Dust Grey — default neutral
- 1015 Hygienic Ivory — see-dirt visual control (dairy/bakery)
- 6011 Reseda Green — wet-process/vegetable wash
- 5024 Pastel Blue — beverage/bottling/water-contact
- 1023 Traffic Yellow — safety/zoning stripes/walkways
- 3009 Oxide Red — meat processing, hides staining

**Certifications:** HACCP; ISEGA (EU 10/2011); NSF/ANSI 51; FDA 21 CFR 175.300; Kosher/Badatz (IL); USDA accepted.

## R5 — MMA Pronto

**Cure modes:**
- Standard: +5 to +25°C, BPO 2%, walk 1h, full 2-3h
- Fast: +15 to +35°C, BPO 1%, walk 45m, full 2h
- **Cold (KILLER FEATURE): -30 to +5°C, BPO 3-4%, walk 1.5h, full 3h**

**Sikafloor Pronto SKU map:**
- Primer: Sikafloor-13/14/15 Pronto (mineral / damp / non-porous)
- Body SL 2-5mm: Sikafloor-24 PurCem Pronto SL; Sikafloor-100 SL Pronto
- Broadcast BC: Sikafloor-61 BC Pronto (quartz scatter binder)
- Topcoat clear: Sikafloor-18 Pronto (~0.3mm); Sikafloor-71 TC Pronto
- Topcoat pigmented: Sikafloor-53 TC Pronto (~12 standard RAL)
- Cove-base: Sikafloor-100 PAS Pronto (thixotropic vertical, R50/75/100)
- Repair patch: Sikafloor-17 RS Pronto (polymer-concrete 6-40mm depth, overnight)

**Broadcast quartz blends (food-industry color names):** Freezer Grey, Kitchen Cream, Dairy Beige, Cold-Lab Blue, Industrial Anthracite, Bakery Sand, Retail Terrazzo (mixed flake decorative).

**Sizes:** 0.4-0.8 / 0.7-1.2 / 1.2-1.8 / 1.8-2.4mm. Density: light 40-60% / full ≥95%.

**Marking:** RAL 1023 yellow zone; 9016 white grid; 3020 red safety; 5015 pedestrian.

**Competitors IL:** Carmit Mr.Fix MMA (local). Global: Stonhard Stonblend MMA, Evonik Degadur, BASF Acure, Flowcrete Peran MMA, Silikal R17.

**Repair mode toggle (overnight patch — killer use case for retail/hospital/food line).**

**Ventilation:** strong acrylic/methacrylate odor during cure; required forced extraction + isolation tent + vapor PPE; occupancy return after full cure + 30min air exchange.

**Color palette:** Sikafloor-53 TC pigmented = ~12 RAL standard; Sikafloor-24 SL pigment paste = full RAL on-site.

## R6 — Microtopping (Pandomo)

**Brand systems:** Pandomo Loft (Ardex/Henkel DE — premium reference); Topciment (ES — Sttandard/Microbase/Microdeck/Microfino); Affor (IT); Mapei Ultratop Loft; Sika MonoTop-723 N; Microcement Beton (RU).

**Thickness:** 2-3mm total (2-coat). Max seamless ~80m² (control joint mandatory beyond, per Pandomo TDS). >5mm risks telegraph cracking.

**Substrate prep:** epoxy 2K primer (Ardex P82, Topciment Primacem Plus); fiberglass mesh 4×4mm in scratch coat (prevents substrate-crack telegraphing — CRITICAL); moisture max 4% CM.

**Trowel patterns (deliberate aesthetic):**
- smooth_loft — Pandomo Loft signature, flat satin, minimal cloud
- cloud — Topciment "Nuvolato", irregular soft sweep
- wave — directional brush, parallel strokes
- mineral — layered cloudy 2 tones, depth
- stone — mottled river-stone mimic
- concrete_effect — raw cement look, Mapei Ultratop Loft
- metallic — mica/bronze pigment, Affor Metal

**Sealer (deepens base color ~10-15%):**
- matte: PU 2K matte (Topciment Topsealer WT Matt)
- satin: PU 2K satin (Pandomo SP-PU Satin) — DEFAULT, most forgiving
- gloss: PU 2K gloss (Sikafloor 304W Gloss)
- wax: traditional wax-cement, low traffic only, re-wax annually
- wet: PU 2K oleophobic R10 (bathroom DIN 51097 class B/C, +sand additive)

**Surface:** floor / wall / counter (continuity is the selling point).

**Pandomo color palette (10 std + custom):** cream PA-W2; sand PA-K3; stone PA-K6; pampas PA-K4 taupe; sahara PA-T5 warm sand; clay PA-T7 terracotta-grey; terracotta PA-T9 burnt orange-clay; olive PA-G4; sea_mist PA-B3 cool pale blue-grey; charcoal PA-K12.

**Compat:** underfloor heating yes (max 28°C surface, ramp 2°C/day during cure); acoustic underlay yes (Schlüter Ditra under primer).

**Defects:** telegraph crack (mesh skipped); efflorescence (moisture >4%); pinhole (over-mixed air); batch variance (mix all bags wet, never per-coat).

## R7 — EPDM Rubber

**Brand systems:** Conipur (BASF DE, IAAF), Mondo (IT IAAF/FIBA Sportflex Super X), Polytan (DE FIH/IAAF), Domo (IT FIH), Decoflex (poured PU indoor sport), Soflex (IL local).

**Material types:**
- poured_EPDM_PU (2-layer SBR base + EPDM wear, PU-bound, in-situ)
- poured_comfortfloor (PU self-level, flexible, indoor)
- prefab_sheet (rolled vulcanized Mondo/Domo)
- interlocking_tile (50×50 EPDM tile, gym/playground)
- SBR_only (recycled black, base/cheap-outdoor)
- TPV (premium UV-stable, ~2× cost)

**Granule colors (EPDM 1-3mm or 1-4mm):** brick red, terracotta, ochre yellow, grass green, sky blue, navy, slate grey, white, black, sand beige.

**Patterns:**
- solid — single tone
- blend_2tone — 60% base + 40% accent, irregular mottle (default playground)
- blend_multi — 3-5 colors random mottle, no zones
- island — distinct color zones (red play-area inside green field)
- graphic — hopscotch/numbers/sport-markings inlaid as cut EPDM
- sport_lines — IAAF/FIBA line markings on solid base

**Thickness tiers:**
- Sport: track IAAF 13-15mm; basketball 8-10; tennis 4-5; gym weight 15-20; cardio 4-6.
- Playground EN 1177 HIC<1000: fall 0.6m→25mm; 1.0m→40mm; 1.4m→55mm; 1.8m→80mm; 2.5m→100-120mm.

**Sport modes:** none; track; court_basketball; court_tennis; hockey; gym_weight; gym_cardio; playground.

**Drainage:** permeable_open_cell (outdoor); sealed (indoor gym).
**Topcoat:** none; PU sealer matte/satin (indoor only).

**Visual realism:** irregular speckle, NOT geometric zones. Granules visible at <2m view.

## R8 — Audit findings (errors + gaps + preset inconsistencies)

### Errors to fix
- "саргелим" plural transliteration wrong → use "саргели/sargel" (singular Hebrew סרגל = ruler/strip)
- broadcast.fine "R10" wrong — sealed fine quartz tests R9, not R10
- broadcast.heavy "R13" wrong — R13 only achievable on exposed broadcast, not sealed
- roughness.coarse-r12 duplicates broadcast axis — slip class belongs to ONE axis
- purcem.primer "Без праймера (на влажный бетон)" wrong — PurCem requires SSD substrate + scratch coat, not primer-free
- terrazzo buildup primer "SikaTop-77 D" labeled as "праймер" — should be "scratch/bond coat" (адгезионная шликер-обмазка)
- mma topcoat "Sikafloor-18 Pronto · 2 × 0.1 мм" wrong — single coat ~0.3mm
- concrete primer "Литий-силикатный упрочнитель · Sikafloor ProSeal-22" wrong product — ProSeal-22 is topical seal/cure-and-seal NOT Li densifier; correct Li densifier = Sikafloor ProSeal W or Prosoco Consolideck LS
- finish list mixes mechanical+chemical: "polished" applies only to concrete/terrazzo (mechanical), not epoxy resinous
- preset.warehouse roughness=R11 wrong for reach-truck (destroys PU wheels) — should be R10
- preset.metallic sets flecks=metallic on epoxy-light but epoxy-light.controls didn't include flecks until v6 (verify)
- preset.cleanroom sets ESD badge on epoxy-light but ESD certification control is on epoxy industrial only
- preset.gym thickness=9mm rubber insufficient — EN 14904 indoor sport requires shock-pad ≥10-14mm
- preset.playground thickness=55mm = HIC 2m max, but most school equipment needs 80mm (HIC 3m)

### Gaps (missing controls/options)
- Chemical resistance / VOC certification (EC1+, AgBB) — for schools/hospitals
- Crack-bridging / elasticity axis (rigid vs SL-flex / ComfortFloor) — car-park decks, roof terraces
- Slope-to-drain / fall axis (1-2%) — every food-plant spec
- Temperature class HF/MF/LP/HM thermal-shock rating —40 to +120°C — purcem
- Densifier-type axis (Li/Na/K silicate) — concrete
- Joint-filler axis (semi-rigid PU/PE) — concrete
- Diamond-grind sequence (50/100/200/400/800/1500/3000) + divider-strip pitch — terrazzo
- Impact-attenuation HIC certification axis distinct from thickness, binder ratio (PU 18% vs 22%) — rubber
- Application temperature axis (down to -25°C — whole point) — mma
- Substrate-moisture (CM%) and pull-off (≥1.5 MPa) acceptance axis — all resin systems
- ESD/surface-resistance class (10⁶/10⁹ Ω) selectable — currently only as cleanroom preset badge
