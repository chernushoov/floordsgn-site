## Рекомендованный Sika system per material page

### Industrial SL Эпокси (склад / гараж / производство)
- Primer: **Sikafloor-161**
- Body: **Sikafloor-263 SL** (2–3 мм, ~1.5 кг/м²/мм, pot life 25 мин, cure 12 ч walk / 7 d full, ~95 МПа)
- Optional sealer: **Sikafloor-264** roller-coat
- Cleanroom alternative: **Sikafloor-269 CR** (ultra-low VOC/AMC)
- Цена IL: **180–260 ₪/м²** installed

### Decorative ComfortFloor (designer / residential / showroom)
- Primer: **Sikafloor-161**
- Body: **Sika ComfortFloor PS-23** (2.4–3 мм, elastic PU, ~3.0 кг/м²)
- Topcoat: **Sikafloor-305 W** water-based PU
- Flake вариант: **PS-25** (with vinyl chips broadcast)
- Ergonomic: **PS-65 / PS-67** (с 5 мм recycled-PU pad, 7–8 мм system)
- Цена IL: **320–450 ₪/м²** installed

### ESD / Conductive
- Conductive primer: **Sikafloor-220 W Conductive** (waterborne epoxy, copper-grid grounding)
- Body: **Sikafloor-262 AS N** (2–3 мм SL conductive, ~95 МПа)
- Premium ESD: **Sikafloor-235 ESD** (2 мм SL)
- Use: server rooms, electronics, hospitals, ammo storage

### PU-cement (food / beverage / heavy chemical)
- Primer: integral OR **Sikafloor-156**
- **Sikafloor-21 PurCem** (smooth, 4–6 мм, >50 МПа) — wet bottling, dairy CIP
- **Sikafloor-21 N PurCem** (satin, 4–9 мм) — food processing, freezers
- **Sikafloor-22 N PurCem** (heavy-duty, 6–12 мм, >60 МПа) — abattoirs, fish, dairy
- Cove-base: **Sikafloor PurCem MT-19** (6–12 мм, hygienic coving)
- Расход: ~1.9–2.0 кг/м²/мм, pot life 15 мин, cure 6 ч walk / 5–7 d full
- Цена IL: **380–550 ₪/м²** installed
- HACCP-International certified, EU 10/2011 food contact

### MMA Fast-Cure (cold storage / overnight install)
- Primer: **Sikafloor-41 P Pronto** (low-vis) или **51 P Pronto** (для пористого бетона)
- Body: **Sikafloor-61 BC Pronto** (broadcast quartz/flake) или **100 SL Pronto** (SL филлер)
- Topcoat: **Sikafloor-71 TC Pronto** (clear) или **53 TC Pronto** (pigmented)
- Cove: **Sikafloor-100 PAS Pronto** (thixotropic vertical)
- Repair / patch: **Sikafloor-17 RS Pronto** (PMMA polymer concrete, 6–40 мм)
- Pot life: 10–15 мин, cure: 1 ч walk-on, 2 ч full
- Working temp: до –30°C

### Terrazzo
- Эпокси-binder: **Sikafloor Terrazzo EM-10** (9–15 мм, 22–24 кг/м²/см, ~80 МПа, cure 24 ч walk / 7 d)
- Cement-based: **Sikafloor Terrazzo CS-31** (15–30 мм, classic)
- Coarse aggregate: **Sikafloor Terrazzo CS-32** (20–40 мм, palladiana-style)
- Aggregate mix: **Sikafloor-001 Aggregate Mix** (marble / glass / mirror)
- Binder для site-cast: **SikaScreed-45**
- Подложка primer: **Sikafloor-156**

### Microtopping / Decorative Overlay
- Sika не имеет прямого "microtopping" SKU
- Cement overlay: **Sikafloor-470 Level** (5–50 мм, fiber-reinforced) + sealer **Sikafloor ProSeal-22**
- Designer epoxy alternative: **Sikafloor DecoDur Metallic FX** (1.5–2 мм, pigmented epoxy)
- DecoDur Flake: **Sikafloor DecoDur EB-Flake** (1.5–2.5 мм, vinyl chips broadcast)

### Polished Concrete
- Dry-shake hardener (на свежей плите): **Sikafloor MetalTop-300** (5–6 кг/м², metallic aggregate, >70 МПа)
- Cure & seal: **Sikafloor ProSeal-22** (solvent-based) или **ProSeal W** (low-VOC water)
- Liquid densifier: литий-силикат (через Gilar special order)

### Rubber / Ergonomic
- Sika не делает rubber sheet/tile сам по себе
- Закрытие задачи: **ComfortFloor PS-65 / PS-67** с 5 мм recycled-PU pad — даёт acoustic + ergonomic
- Wet sport: **Sikafloor PurCem** + rubber granules option

### Restoration / Recoat
- Substrate patch: **Sika MonoTop-615** (R3 repair, 5–50 мм) или **MonoTop-4012** (high-build, 6–120 мм)
- Heavy structural: **Sika MonoTop-4100 Protect** (R4-class, 10–80 мм)
- Joint bridging: **Sikadur-Combiflex SG** + **Sikadur-31 Hi-Mod Gel**
- Crack injection: **Sikadur-31** (epoxy gel, 1.6 кг/л)
- Recoat thin epoxy: **Sikafloor-264** или **Sikafloor-169** (clear)

### Joint Sealants
- **Sikaflex PRO-3** (1K PU, traffic-grade, 6–35 мм)
- **Sikaflex-1a** (general construction joints, 6–25 мм)

### Waterproofing (под полом)
- Pre-applied membrane: **SikaProof A+ 12** (FPO sheet, 1.7 мм)
- Liquid bitumen: **Sika Igolflex-365** (rubberized emulsion) или **Igolflex-620** (thixotropic)

## Trust Signals для surfacing на сайте

### Стандарты соответствия
- **EN 13813** — synthetic resin screed CE marking (Sikafloor-161, -263 SL, -169, PurCem, MMA Pronto)
- **EN 1504-2 / EN 1504-3** — concrete protection/repair compliance (Sikafloor coatings, MonoTop)
- **EN 1186 / EU 10/2011** — food-contact (PurCem, select эпокси для food/beverage)
- **HACCP-International certified** — PurCem food-grade systems
- **EPD (Environmental Product Declaration)** — verified to EN 15804 + ISO 14025, published via IBU. Available for ComfortFloor PS-23, Sikafloor-264, PurCem
- **LEED v4.1** — low-VOC формулировки для indoor air quality credits (-269 CR, PS-23, ProSeal W)
- **ISO 9001 / 14001 / 45001** — corporate Sika certifications
- **DIN 51130 R-class** slip ratings (R9–R13 в зависимости от системы)
- **Israeli Standard SI-5566** (synthetic floor coverings) — соответствие через EN 13813

### Гарантия
Sika offers **system warranty только** при установке certified Sika applicator-ом. FloorDSGN qualifies.
- 5-yr material+workmanship для industrial systems
- 10-yr для PurCem food
