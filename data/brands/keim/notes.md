## Who they are

KEIM is a German mineral-coatings manufacturer founded **1878** by Adolf Wilhelm Keim in Diedorf, Bavaria. KEIM is the original inventor of **mineral silicate paint** — a chemistry that chemically bonds to mineral substrates (masonry, render, concrete) via silicification, rather than physically adhering like acrylic. The result is a coating that is highly vapour-permeable, chemically inert, UV-stable, and lasts decades without recoating.

The brand is the global reference for mineral silicate paints. Where Sika is to flooring, KEIM is to mineral wall coatings. Their 140+ year reference list includes museums, listed buildings, historic façade restorations across Europe.

KEIM is **owner-named as a partner** for FloorDSGN (per project memory), suggesting owner has direct contact with KEIM's IL representation or has worked with their products on floor-adjacent projects.

## Product hierarchy

### Exterior façade (the historic flagship)
- **KEIM Soldalit®** — multi-substrate mineral silicate. Works on mineral AND organic (acrylic) substrates. The product to specify when an existing acrylic-painted façade needs to be overcoated with mineral chemistry without stripping.
- **KEIM Granital®** — pure mineral silicate for mineral substrates only. Historic flagship.

### Interior
- **KEIM Innostar®** — interior mineral silicate paint. AgBB-certified low-VOC, non-allergenic, breathable. Used in healthcare / schools / residential where indoor air quality matters.
- **KEIM Concretal®** — decorative concrete-look mineral coating. Architectural / showroom application.
- **KEIM Design-Lasur** — translucent decorative glazes (lasures).

### Restoration
- **KEIM Restauro line** — heritage-restoration silicate paints for listed buildings, museums, monuments. The product chosen specifically because acrylic paint on historic substrate traps moisture and accelerates damage.
