# Altro — brand profile

## Who they are

Altro is a UK safety-vinyl specialist founded 1919, headquartered in Letchworth Garden City. They are a family-run company (Bertram family — fourth-generation), one of the most-cited safety vinyl manufacturers in commercial / institutional construction worldwide.

Altro's marketing pitch is **lifetime PTV warranty** — the slip-resistance (Pendulum Test Value) of an Altro safety vinyl floor is guaranteed for the life of the product. This is mechanically achieved by embedding silicon carbide / aluminum oxide grains throughout the full thickness of the vinyl sheet, so wear does not expose a smooth substrate. The PTV ≥ 36 wet threshold (UK HSE recommendation for sloped surfaces and wet shod traffic) is maintained over 15+ years of foot traffic.

This is the genuine differentiator versus sheet vinyl from Forbo, Tarkett, Mannington — those are decorative vinyls without through-thickness grain. Altro is what you specify when the spec calls out "R12 + PTV maintenance" or "lifetime slip warranty."

## Product hierarchy

### Safety vinyl — shod (DIN 51130 R-class)
- **Altro Stronghold 30** — 3.0 mm, R12, kitchens / food prep / butchery. The flagship.
- **Altro Classic 25** — 2.5 mm, general commercial.
- **Altro XpressLay** — adhesive-free, fast retrofit, easy uplift.
- **Altro Suprema II** — decorative premium variant; safety + aesthetic.
- **Altro Wood Safety** — wood-look safety vinyl.

### Safety vinyl — barefoot (DIN 51097 A/B/C)
- **Altro Aquarius** — Class B; showers, changing rooms, pool surrounds.

### Marine
- **Altro Marine 20** — 2.0 mm, IMO-compliant fire spec, boat / ship decks.

### Transportation (Transflor range)
- **Altro Transflor Artis™** — decorative transit flooring.
- **Altro Transflor Metris™** — performance variant.
- **Altro Transflor Sonis™** — acoustic variant.

### Wall cladding (Whiterock)
- **Altro Whiterock** — hygienic vertical wall cladding companion. Floor + wall single-supplier system for kitchens / healthcare / labs.

## What this means for our positioning

Altro is **not a competitor to Sika / Mapei / MasterTop on industrial epoxy** — it is a different floor category. For floordsgn.com:

1. **Safety vinyl is the answer** when the project is healthcare-corridor / commercial-kitchen back-of-house / retail / education where the buyer wants the cleanability of a vinyl sheet plus mandated slip safety. Altro is the named brand.
2. **PTV lifetime warranty** is a real engineering claim, not marketing. Mention it where slip-injury liability is part of the buyer's risk model.
3. **Whiterock + Altro floor combo** is the named answer in our "hospital corridor" and "commercial kitchen" use cases for projects that need cove-base + wall hygiene in addition to the floor.
4. **For HACCP wet food production** Altro is the simpler vinyl alternative to PU-cement. Altro Stronghold 30 R12 + Whiterock walls is cheaper to install than PurCem 22N HD but with a shorter warranty horizon (15-year vinyl vs 25-year PurCem).
