# Polyflor — brand profile

**Source data:** Polyflor homepage + commercial section + safety category page fetched 2026-05-27 (cookie session required). 13 Polysafe SKUs + 4 other product family members catalogued by direct URL.

## Who they are

Polyflor is a UK vinyl flooring manufacturer founded 1915 in Manchester. They are owned by **James Halstead plc** — a UK-listed company. Polyflor is the most established direct competitor to Altro in the UK safety-vinyl market, with broadly equivalent technical depth, broader decorative ranges, and generally lower pricing.

Polyflor's market position is **the price-competitive Altro alternative** — when the buyer wants R10–R12 PTV-rated safety vinyl without paying the Altro lifetime-warranty premium. The two brands appear side-by-side in nearly every UK schools / hospitals / commercial kitchens spec.

## Product hierarchy

### Polysafe — the safety vinyl flagship line
Polyflor's main safety vinyl family. PUR-treated for cleanability, PTV-rated wet, available in 13+ variants:

- **Polysafe Apex 55** — 3.0 mm flagship, heavy-duty institutional.
- **Polysafe Hydro / Hydro Evolve** — barefoot wet zones (showers, pool surrounds). DIN 51097 Class B.
- **Polysafe Verona PUR** — decorative premium (Original + Pure Colours).
- **Polysafe Quattro PUR** — general commercial wet-shod.
- **Polysafe Wood fx PUR** — wood-look safety vinyl.
- **Polysafe Stone fx PUR** — stone-look safety vinyl.
- **Polysafe Ultima** — premium tier.
- **Polysafe QuickLay PUR** — adhesive-free (Polyflor's competitor to Altro XpressLay).
- **Polysafe Standard PUR** — entry tier.

### Decorative vinyl families
- **Expona Flow PUR** — homogeneous vinyl, single-layer, design-focused.
- **Forest fx PUR** — wood-effect commercial vinyl.
- **Acoustix Forest fx PUR** — wood-effect with integrated acoustic backing.
- **Designatex PUR / Architex PUR** — decorative ranges.

### Specialty
- **Voyager Transport Flooring** — public transit (bus / train / metro).
- **Wall Cladding** — Polyflor's hygienic wall cladding companion.
- **LVT (Luxury Vinyl Tiles)** — Camaro / Affinity / Expona LVT ranges.
