## Who they are

Stonhard is a US-based resinous flooring specialist headquartered in Maple Shade, NJ, founded 1922. They are part of **RPM International Inc.** (Tremco's parent) — putting them in the same corporate family as Tremco CPG (Flowcrete). Stonhard's distinctive business model is **single-source warranty**: the same company manufactures the material, installs the floor (through trained Stonhard-employed crews), and underwrites the warranty. This is genuinely different from the standard model where a manufacturer sells to a distributor who sells to an applicator — in Stonhard's model there is no party to blame between the material and the install.

For high-stakes specifications (pharma, food processing, semiconductor) the single-source warranty is a real differentiator. It is also why Stonhard's pricing premium versus Sika/Mapei/MasterTop is justifiable in spec — the buyer is paying for warranty consolidation, not just the material.

## Product hierarchy

Stonhard organizes its product portfolio under the "Ston-" prefix:

### Stonclad — high-strength industrial flagship
Epoxy and urethane heavy-duty resin for abrasion / impact / chemical resistance in aggressive spaces. Known industry SKUs: **Stonclad UT** (urethane troweled, food/industrial), **Stonclad HT** (heat resistant), **Stonclad GS** (general service).

### Stonshield — textured slip-resistant epoxy/urethane
Safe, easy-to-clean systems with design flexibility. Slip-resistant broadcast finishes. Food prep, kitchens, locker rooms.

### Stonblend — decorative seamless smooth
Smooth stain-resistant decorative seamless flooring for showroom / retail / commercial aesthetic spaces.

### Stoncrete — decorative epoxy mortar (micro-terrazzo look)
Epoxy-based micro terrazzo with aggregate options (quartz, recycled glass). High-traffic decorative system, direct competitor of Sikafloor EM-10 and Flowcrete Mondéco.

### Stoncrete EFX — premium decorative aggregates
Stoncrete's premium decorative aggregate variant. Visible TDS list shows C-2 Amber, C-2 Mirror, C-2 NJ Quartz aggregate finishes. Often paired with **Stonseal CF7** topcoat.

### Stonchem — chemical-resistant lining
High-performance resins for ultra-corrosive environments. The Stonhard equivalent of Sikafloor-381 Novolac epoxy and vinyl-ester systems. Known SKUs: **Stonchem 801, 802, 821, 830**. For acid bunds, plating zones, battery rooms.

### Stonkote — thin-film coatings
Resinous floor coatings line. Thin-film protection for commercial/industrial floors. Competes with Sikafloor-264 / MultiDur thin-film products.
