## Who they are

Mapei is an Italian construction-chemicals multinational founded 1937 in Milan, family-owned (Squinzi family). Mapei is structurally similar to Sika and Master Builders — a broad construction-chemicals portfolio across mortars, adhesives, sealants, concrete admixtures, waterproofing, and flooring. Within flooring they operate as **Mapefloor**.

Mapei's standing is as the European-Italian alternative to Sika. Where Sika has Swiss / German engineering credibility, Mapei has Italian-design + comprehensive-portfolio credibility. They are consistently in the top-3 flooring brand specs in Europe (with Sika and the now-Sika-owned Ucrete).

The Italian heritage makes Mapei particularly strong in southern-European markets — Italy, Spain, Greece, plus North Africa and Middle East including Israel. Mapei has an established Israeli importer (need to confirm specific company name).

## Product hierarchy

### Mapefloor — industrial flooring portfolio

- **Mapefloor I 302 SL** — 100% solids epoxy self-leveling 2–3 mm. Workhorse industrial SL, direct competitor of Sikafloor-263 SL and MasterTop 1235SL.
- **Mapefloor I 320** — trowel-applied epoxy mortar 6–12 mm. For heavy industrial: vehicle workshops, dock levellers, forklift bays. Competes with Sikafloor-31 EpoCem mortar and Stonhard Stonclad HT.
- **Mapefloor PU 410** — flexible PU flooring. Competes with Sikafloor Monoflex / ComfortFloor PU.
- **Mapefloor CPU/HD** — polyurethane cement HD for food / pharma. Mapei's direct equivalent of Ucrete UD200 and Sikafloor PurCem 22N HD.

### Planitop — companion repair mortars

Mapei's repair-mortar line, frequently specified in the same project as a Mapefloor system when substrate needs work:

- **Planitop 400** — EN 1504-3 R3 polymer-modified cementitious repair. General-purpose.
- **Planitop 23** — thin patching variant.
- **Planitop HDM** — EN 1504-3 R4 high-strength structural repair.

### Epojet / Eporip — injection epoxies

- **Mapei Epojet LV** — low-viscosity crack injection epoxy. Direct competitor of Sikadur-52 LP.
- **Mapei Eporip** — non-shrink fluid epoxy for sealing cracks before applying overlays. Common in microcement overlay specs.

### Bioblock® — silver-ion antimicrobial

This is Mapei's signature differentiator. **Mapefloor Bioblock®** is a silver-ion (Ag+) antimicrobial additive integrated into the flooring resin matrix, with published Mapei research showing reduced MRSA colonisation in hospital corridor environments. Competitors include Polygiene® (used by Flowcrete Flowfresh in their PU-cement systems). Often specified in healthcare with Sikafloor MultiDur ES-26 SL.

## What this means for our positioning

Mapei is the **European alternative** to Sika in our industrial floor articles. On a comparison page:

- **Sika** = Swiss/German engineering brand, broad portfolio, dominant in IL.
- **Mapei** = Italian alternative, equally comprehensive portfolio, Italian-design heritage, strong in southern Europe + Middle East.
- **Master Builders / Ucrete** = now a Sika sub-brand after 2023 acquisition.
- **Flowcrete / Stonhard** = niche specialists for specific use cases (Polygiene-loaded PU-cement for Flowcrete; single-source warranty for Stonhard).

For our brand-profile page at `articles/brands/mapei-mapefloor.html` we should highlight:

1. **Bioblock antimicrobial** — Mapei's unique competitive moat. The only major brand with documented silver-ion in their flooring matrix outside Flowcrete's licensed Polygiene.
2. **Italian-design credibility** — for IL designers / architects who specify on aesthetic provenance, Mapei carries the "Italian-engineered" cachet alongside Mapei's actual technical depth.
3. **Companion repair line (Planitop / Epojet)** — Mapei is the "one-stop" specifier's brand for projects that need both substrate repair AND new flooring. The system can be one supplier from prep to topcoat.
