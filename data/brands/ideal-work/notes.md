# Ideal Work — brand profile

**Source data:** 10 product pages fetched 2026-05-27 from idealwork.com. No TDS PDFs publicly accessible — Ideal Work's site does not expose technical data sheets to anonymous visitors; specs come from product-page copy and FAQ. **Status:** my synthesis from publicly visible product pages.

## Who they are

Ideal Work is an Italian manufacturer of decorative concrete and microcement systems, headquartered in Sarmede (Veneto, Italy), founded around 2000. They position themselves as the originators of Microtopping® — a thin (2–4 mm) cementitious decorative floor coating — and a broader portfolio of Venetian terrazzo, pebbled aggregate, and stencil floor systems.

The branding is consistently European-design-led: minimalist photography, hospitality/residential case studies, multilingual (Italian / English / French / Spanish). Their go-to-market is consultative — find an Ideal Work applicator network member, schedule a design visit, then specify. This is materially different from Topciment's approach (download a TDS, mix on site).

## Product hierarchy

### 1. Microtopping® — flagship 3 mm decorative coating
- Cement-polymer coating, not a resin
- Thickness 2–4 mm total system
- Two application approaches (Base Coat ×2 + Finish Coat, or Base Coat ×2 + HP Coat)
- 29 colours available
- Indoor + exterior
- No expansion joints required
- Drying: 3–4 hours per coat at 20°C, 60% RH; 12–18 h before sealer
- Walk-on: 48 h after final application
- Full hardness: 7 days
- Throughput: ~100 m² per week

### 2. Microcement — sibling product
A separate microcement product listed alongside Microtopping. Specs overlap with Microtopping (2–3 mm thickness, cement-polymer). Likely targets a slightly different finish aesthetic or applicator tier; their public pages don't sharply differentiate the two.

### 3. Lixio® / Lixio®+ — Venetian terrazzo
Modern Venetian-terrazzo system. **Lixio** is the original; **Lixio+** uses larger aggregates (5–12 mm) and an updated application technique, with slightly increased floor thickness.

### 4. Sassoitalia® Floor — pebbled aggregate
Outdoor pebble/aggregate floor for hard landscaping (squares, driveways, public areas, pools). The brand's outdoor/landscape product line.

### 5. Stenciltop Floor — overlay for slab renovation
Decorative overlay specifically positioned for renovating and beautifying existing slabs. Stencilled patterns, decorative finishes over poured-concrete bases.

### 6. Ideal Wall — vertical decorative
The wall companion product line — same aesthetic language as their floor products, applied as vertical finish.

## What this means for our positioning

Ideal Work is a **decorative microcement specialist** in the same broad segment as Topciment, but the positioning is different:

- **Topciment** = technical-spec, multi-tier product portfolio (2K / 1K / ready-to-use), full TDS transparency, sold globally to professional applicators through technical specs.
- **Ideal Work** = design-led, hospitality/residential-focused, consultative selling, technical info gated behind their applicator network.

For an FloorDSGN reader doing technical comparison, Topciment is more legible. For a reader who has already seen "Microtopping" on a designer's mood-board and is asking about the specific product, Ideal Work is the answer.

Both compete with **Mortex (BEAL)** in the European decorative microcement space, with **Pandomo (Ardex)** and **Marmorino (Stucco Italiano, Meoded, Vasari)** at the more artisanal/Venetian-plaster end.
