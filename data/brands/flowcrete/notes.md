## Who they are

Flowcrete was founded 1982 in Sandbach, Cheshire, UK as a resin flooring specialist — originally focused on industrial seamless floors. Over the decades they grew into one of the three classic European PU-cement reference brands (with Mapei and Master Builders / Ucrete). They are now part of **Tremco CPG** (Tremco Construction Products Group), itself owned by **Stark Holdings**.

Flowcrete operates globally with regional sites: UK, EU multi-language portal, Pakistan / UAE, India, New Zealand. Their MENA presence via flowcrete.ae suggests reasonably active distribution into the Middle East, which is relevant for Israeli sourcing.

## Product hierarchy

### Flowfresh — PU-cement flagship (their unique competitive moat)

The signature Flowcrete product family:

- **Flowfresh HF** — 9 mm heavy-duty PU-cement, R11–R12 broadcast options, thermal cycling -40°C to +120°C. **The critical differentiator:** Flowfresh integrates **Polygiene® silver-ion antimicrobial** directly into the resin matrix. Published Flowcrete research shows reduced bacterial colonisation versus standard PU-cement. This makes Flowfresh the preferred PU-cement for HACCP/FSSC 22000 food-production floors where antimicrobial argument is part of the spec.
- **Flowfresh MF** — 6 mm medium-duty.
- **Flowfresh RT** — 4 mm thin-section / smooth finish.

Flowfresh + Polygiene = the answer to "Sika PurCem doesn't carry an antimicrobial." That single differentiator is why Flowcrete keeps a serious market presence despite Sika owning the volume.

### Industrial epoxy (Flowshield, Peran)
- **Flowshield SL** — 100% solids epoxy SL, direct competitor to Sikafloor-263 SL.
- **Peran SL** — value-tier epoxy SL.
- **Flowcoat SF41** — thin-film epoxy coating (referenced in their defence/nuclear case study).

### Screed (Isocrete, Flowscreed)
- **Isocrete 1500 Plus** — polymer-modified self-leveling screed. Subfloor leveller under Flowfresh/Flowshield finishes. Notable: Flowcrete announced 2025 packaging in 1-tonne bags (volume-pricing for large projects).
- **Flowscreed Industrial Top** — industrial flowing screed.

### Decorative (Mondéco — seamless epoxy terrazzo)
- **Mondéco** family — Flowcrete's seamless epoxy terrazzo 10–12 mm. Direct competitor of Sikafloor EM-10 and Stonhard StoneTreads.

### Car park deck waterproofing (Deckshield)
- **Deckshield ID** — PU-based car park deck waterproofing system. This is Flowcrete's most distinctive non-flooring strength — car park decks are a sector where Flowcrete has more depth than Sika in the UK market.
