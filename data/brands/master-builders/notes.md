## Who they are (and ownership timeline)

**Master Builders Solutions** is the construction-chemicals brand portfolio that traces back to BASF's construction-chemicals division, founded effectively at BASF's acquisitions of MBT (1995), Degussa Construction Chemicals (2006), and consolidated as **BASF Construction Chemicals**. In 2020 BASF sold the division to Lone Star Funds, who rebranded it **MBCC Group** — Master Builders Construction Chemicals. The "Master Builders Solutions" brand is the customer-facing product line within MBCC.

In **2023 Sika AG acquired MBCC Group**. As of 2026, MasterTop and Ucrete are technically Sika products, though the standalone brand still operates with its own catalog and licensed-applicator networks. Sika UK explicitly references the Sika® Ucrete® licensed-applicator program.

For floordsgn.com positioning: treat MasterTop and Ucrete as **legacy-named Sika products** that still appear in older specifications and procurement documents. New specifications may now equally call out the Sika equivalent (PurCem for Ucrete, MultiDur for MasterTop industrial epoxy).

## Product hierarchy

### MasterTop — industrial flooring (epoxy + MMA + Xolutec)

- **MasterTop 1235SL** — 100% solids epoxy self-leveling 2–3 mm. Workhorse warehouse / light-industrial SL. Direct equivalent of Sikafloor-263 SL.
- **MasterTop 1912 Xolutec** — Premium epoxy with MBCC's proprietary "Xolutec" modified chemistry. Marketed as having better chemical resistance and longer pot life than standard 100%-solids epoxy.
- **MasterTop 1817 PC** — MMA (methyl methacrylate) polymer concrete. Fast-cure overnight install. Direct equivalent of Sikafloor Pronto.

### Ucrete — PU-cement HD flagship

- **Ucrete UD200** — The historic industry-reference heavy-duty PU-cement for food production. -40°C to +120°C, point load ≥ 8 N/mm², 9–12 mm thickness. Carries HACCP, FSSC 22000, ISEGA, NSF/ANSI 51, FDA 21 CFR 175.300 certifications. Direct equivalent of Sikafloor PurCem 22N HD.
- **Ucrete MF** — Lighter-grade Ucrete for less-aggressive food zones.
- Various other Ucrete SKUs (UD, HF, RG) for specific application requirements.

Ucrete is one of the three classic "you specify it because the food plant has been running it for 25 years and won't accept alternatives" brand names — alongside **Flowcrete Flowfresh** and **Sikafloor PurCem** (now also Sika).
