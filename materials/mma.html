MMA Resin Flooring

Methyl Methacrylate Systems

Methyl Methacrylate resin systems that cure in hours, not days. When downtime costs more than the floor, MMA delivers — with full traffic capability in just 2-4 hours.

Where MMA Excels

MMA flooring is the solution when conventional systems aren't fast enough. Its unique chemistry allows installation in conditions that would halt other flooring projects.

24/7 Operations

Food processing, pharmaceutical, logistics — facilities that cannot shut down. Weekend or overnight installation.

Cold Storage

Cures at temperatures as low as -30°C. Freezer floors, cold chain facilities, refrigerated warehouses.

Retail Renovation

Mall renovations, supermarket refits — install overnight, open next morning.

Healthcare

Hospital renovations, operating room refurbishment. Minimal odor formulations available.

Speed Without Compromise

MMA's rapid cure doesn't mean shortcuts on preparation. Surface prep follows the same rigorous standards as epoxy systems.

  • Shot blasting — Preferred method for optimal surface profile. Can proceed immediately after
  • Diamond grinding — Alternative where shot blasting isn't feasible
  • Moisture tolerance — More forgiving than epoxy, but excess moisture still requires management
  • Cold conditions — No heating required for sub-zero installations
  • Rapid primer cure — MMA primers cure in 30-60 minutes, enabling same-day system completion

System Architecture

MMA systems follow a similar layered approach to epoxy, but with dramatically faster cure times at each stage.

01

Reactive Primer

MMA-based primer penetrates and seals substrate. Cures in 30-60 minutes at 20°C, proportionally faster at higher temperatures.

02

Body Coat

Self-leveling or broadcast MMA resin. Aggregate broadcast for slip resistance if required. Cures in 1-2 hours.

03

Seal Coat

Clear or pigmented topcoat. UV-stable aliphatic formulations for exterior or high-light applications. Full cure in 1-2 hours.

System Variants

MMA is available in multiple configurations to match your specific application requirements:

Self-Leveling

Smooth, seamless finish. 2-3mm. Rapid installation for minimal downtime.

Quartz Broadcast

Colored quartz for durability and slip resistance. 3-4mm total build.

Anti-Slip

Textured aggregate finish for wet areas, ramps, and exterior applications.

Decorative Flake

Color flake broadcast for aesthetic appeal. Common in retail and commercial.

Key Advantages

  • Rapid Cure — Full traffic in 2-4 hours. Complete projects in a single shift or overnight.
  • Cold Temperature Cure — Installs at -30°C to +35°C. No heating required for cold storage or winter work.
  • UV Stability — Aliphatic MMA doesn't yellow or chalk. Suitable for exterior applications.
  • Chemical Resistance — Excellent resistance to acids, alkalis, and many solvents.
  • Flexibility — More flexible than epoxy, bridges hairline cracks, handles substrate movement.
  • Minimal Downtime — The real advantage — production continues with minimal interruption.

Maintenance Protocol

MMA floors deliver performance comparable to epoxy with significantly faster installation. Maintenance is straightforward.

Daily

Sweep or auto-scrub to remove debris. Spot clean spills as they occur.

Weekly

Machine scrub with neutral pH cleaner. Rinse thoroughly.

Quarterly

Deep clean and inspection. Check high-wear areas for topcoat condition.

As Needed

Rapid recoating possible — MMA topcoat can be applied with minimal prep.
