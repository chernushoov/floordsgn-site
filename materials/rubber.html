Where Rubber Flooring Excels

Rubber flooring provides the perfect combination of durability, comfort, and safety for demanding environments where people stand, move, and work.

Fitness Facilities

Gyms, weight rooms, CrossFit boxes. Impact absorption, dropped weight protection, easy cleaning.

Healthcare

Hospitals, clinics, aged care. Anti-fatigue for staff, slip-resistant, sound absorbing.

Education

Schools, universities, libraries. Durable, quiet, slip-safe. Sustainable recycled options.

Playgrounds & Recreation

Safety surfacing for play areas. Fall-height rated poured rubber systems.

Substrate Preparation

Rubber flooring can be glued, loose-laid, or installed over underlayment. Substrate requirements vary by installation method.

  • Full adhesive — Substrate must be flat (SR1-SR2), dry, and free of contaminants. Best for permanent installations
  • Loose lay — Heavier rubber tiles can be loose-laid. Allows for replacement and access to subfloor
  • Interlocking tiles — No adhesive required. Quick install for gyms and temporary applications
  • Poured rubber — EPDM granules bound with polyurethane. Seamless, any thickness, playground-rated
  • Moisture — Rubber is impermeable; excess moisture must be addressed to prevent mold beneath

Rubber Flooring Types

We offer multiple rubber flooring technologies to match your specific application requirements.

01

Vulcanized Rubber

Factory-produced sheets or tiles. Vulcanization process creates durable, resilient product. Available in natural or synthetic formulations.

02

Recycled Rubber

Made from recycled tires. Available as tiles, rolls, or poured-in-place. Excellent for gyms and playgrounds. Sustainable choice.

03

Poured EPDM

EPDM rubber granules mixed with polyurethane binder, poured on-site. Seamless, any thickness, any color. Ideal for playgrounds and sports surfacing.

Surface Patterns

Different surface textures provide varying levels of slip resistance and aesthetic options:

Smooth

Easy to clean. Common in healthcare and retail. Requires good drainage design for wet areas.

Raised Disc / Stud

Classic rubber flooring look. Enhanced slip resistance. Transit and industrial applications.

Hammered

Textured for slip resistance and visual interest. Hides wear patterns well.

Custom

Logos, patterns, wayfinding integrated into the floor. Design possibilities unlimited.

Key Advantages

  • Comfort & Anti-Fatigue — Cushioned surface reduces strain on legs and back. Essential for standing work environments.
  • Sound Absorption — Significantly reduces impact noise and airborne sound transmission. Quieter spaces.
  • Slip Resistance — Natural grip even when wet. Excellent safety profile for high-traffic areas.
  • Impact Absorption — Protects dropped items and absorbs falls. Critical for gyms and play areas.
  • Sustainable Options — Recycled tire content. Natural rubber from sustainable sources. Long lifespan.
  • Easy Maintenance — Sweep and mop. No waxing required. Resilient surface resists staining.

Maintenance Protocol

Rubber flooring is low-maintenance and resilient. Proper care extends service life and maintains appearance.

Daily

Sweep or vacuum to remove grit. Damp mop high-traffic areas as needed.

Weekly

Machine scrub with neutral pH cleaner. Rinse well to prevent residue buildup.

Monthly

Deep clean and inspect for damage. Address cuts or tears promptly.

Annually

Professional cleaning if needed. Check seams and edges for lifting.
