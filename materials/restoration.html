Floor Restoration & Sealer Renewal

Renew Don't Replace

Extend the life of existing floors through professional restoration, recoating, and sealer renewal. Cost-effective maintenance that restores appearance and protection at a fraction of replacement cost.

What We Restore

Our restoration services cover all major flooring systems. We assess each floor individually to determine the most effective restoration approach.

Epoxy Floor Recoating

Worn epoxy floors recoated with matching or upgraded topcoats. Restores gloss, repairs damage, extends service life.

Polished Concrete Re-Polish

Diamond honing and re-polishing of worn polished concrete. Restores shine without full replacement.

Terrazzo Restoration

Historic or worn terrazzo ground and polished back to original condition. Crack repair included.

Microtopping Resealing

Worn sealer stripped and renewed. Restores stain resistance and appearance.

Assessment Process

Not all floors can be restored. We conduct thorough assessment to determine if restoration is viable and cost-effective before recommending a course of action.

  • Adhesion testing — Pull-off tests verify existing coating adhesion. Delaminated coatings may need removal
  • Damage assessment — Extent of wear, chemical damage, mechanical damage documented
  • Moisture testing — Underlying moisture issues must be addressed before recoating
  • Compatibility — New coatings must be compatible with existing system. Unknown coatings may need removal
  • Cost-benefit analysis — We will tell you honestly if replacement makes more sense than restoration

Restoration Workflow

Our systematic approach ensures consistent, high-quality restoration results.

01

Assessment & Quote

Site visit, testing, documentation. We provide detailed report with options and honest recommendations. No-obligation quote.

02

Preparation

Diamond grinding or sanding to create profile for new coating. Repairs to cracks, chips, and damaged areas. Thorough cleaning.

03

Restoration

Application of primer (if needed), body coats, and topcoats. System matched to existing floor or upgraded as specified.

Common Restoration Scenarios

Here are typical situations where restoration provides excellent value:

Warehouse Traffic Wear

High-traffic lanes recoated. Edge wear addressed. Demarcation lines renewed.

Kitchen Sealer Failure

Stripped and resealed after chemical damage or wear. Non-slip texture maintained.

Showroom Refresh

Scratched or dull finish polished and resealed. Restores retail appearance.

Historic Terrazzo

Decades-old terrazzo ground, polished, and sealed. Reveals original beauty.

Key Advantages

  • Cost Effective — 50-70% of replacement cost. Extends floor life at fraction of new installation price.
  • Less Downtime — Typically 1-3 days vs. 1-2 weeks for full replacement. Business continues faster.
  • Sustainable — Keeps existing floor out of landfill. Minimal new materials required.
  • No Demolition — No removal of existing floor. Less disruption, noise, and waste.
  • Upgrade Opportunity — Can upgrade to better topcoat, add slip resistance, or change color during restoration.
  • Preventive Maintenance — Regular recoating prevents wear-through to substrate. Extends total floor lifespan.

Maintenance Planning

The best time to restore is before the floor fails completely. We help you plan maintenance cycles for optimal floor performance.

Annual Inspection

We assess floor condition and recommend maintenance. Catches issues early.

High-Traffic Recoating

Recoat wear areas every 3-5 years to maintain protection before substrate exposure.

Full Recoat Cycle

Complete topcoat renewal every 5-10 years depending on traffic and use.

Maintenance Contracts

We offer annual maintenance agreements for planned, budgeted floor care.
