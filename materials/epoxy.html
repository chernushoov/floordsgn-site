Epoxy Floor Systems

High-Performance Seamless Coating

Two-component resin systems engineered for maximum chemical resistance, mechanical durability, and seamless hygiene. The benchmark for industrial and commercial flooring across Israel.

Where Epoxy Performs

Epoxy flooring excels in demanding industrial environments where chemical exposure, heavy traffic, and strict hygiene requirements are the norm. From manufacturing plants to pharmaceutical facilities, epoxy delivers uncompromising performance.

Manufacturing Facilities

Heavy machinery, forklift traffic, oil and coolant exposure. Self-leveling or broadcast systems 3-5mm.

Warehouses & Logistics

High-traffic zones, pallet jack resistance, demarcation lines. Broadcast quartz systems for durability.

Food Processing

HACCP-compliant, antimicrobial options, coved skirting. Urethane-modified for thermal shock resistance.

Pharmaceutical & Cleanrooms

ESD-control, seamless finish, chemical resistance. ISO-classified environments.

Substrate Preparation

Epoxy systems bond mechanically and chemically to the substrate. Proper preparation is non-negotiable for long-term performance. Our team ensures every surface is optimally prepared.

  • Shot blasting — Creates optimal surface profile (CSP 3-5) and removes laitance
  • Diamond grinding — For thinner systems or where shot blasting isn't possible
  • Crack repair — Structural cracks filled with flexible epoxy, hairline cracks with penetrating primer
  • Moisture testing — Calcium chloride and RH probe testing per ASTM F1869/F2170
  • Contamination removal — Oil, grease, curing compounds must be completely removed

System Architecture

Every epoxy installation follows a precise multi-layer system, each component engineered to work together for maximum performance and longevity.

01

Primer Coat

Low-viscosity epoxy penetrates substrate pores, consolidates weak surface layers, and provides chemical bond for subsequent coats. Coverage: 0.15-0.25 kg/m².

02

Body Coat / Broadcast Layer

Self-leveling or troweled epoxy mortar builds thickness. For high-traffic areas, quartz aggregate broadcast into wet resin creates mechanical interlock and wear resistance.

03

Seal Coat / Topcoat

Clear or pigmented topcoat encapsulates aggregate, provides UV stability (aliphatic systems), and determines final gloss level and chemical resistance.

System Variants

We offer multiple epoxy system configurations to match your specific requirements:

Self-Leveling

Smooth, seamless finish. 2-3mm thickness. Ideal for light-medium traffic, clean environments.

Quartz Broadcast

Colored quartz aggregate for texture and durability. 3-5mm. Heavy traffic, decorative options.

Mortar System

High-build troweled system. 6-12mm. Extreme impact and thermal shock resistance.

ESD/Conductive

Electrostatic discharge control. Electronics manufacturing, data centers, operating rooms.

Key Advantages

  • Chemical Resistance — Resistant to acids, alkalis, solvents, oils, and fuels. FDA-compliant options available.
  • Seamless & Hygienic — No joints or grout lines where bacteria can harbor. Easy to clean and sanitize.
  • Mechanical Durability — High compressive strength (80+ MPa), impact resistance, and abrasion resistance.
  • Design Flexibility — Unlimited color options, metallic effects, flake systems, logos, and demarcation.
  • Fast Return to Service — Light foot traffic in 24 hours, full mechanical load in 7 days.
  • Long Service Life — 10-15+ years in industrial environments with proper maintenance.

Maintenance Protocol

Epoxy floors are low-maintenance but not no-maintenance. A simple cleaning protocol extends service life significantly:

Daily

Dust mop or auto-scrubber to remove abrasive particles. Spot clean spills immediately.

Weekly

Wet mop with neutral pH cleaner (diluted). Avoid acidic or alkaline cleaners.

Quarterly

Deep clean with auto-scrubber. Inspect for damage, especially at joints and edges.

Annually

Professional inspection. Recoat high-wear areas as needed to maintain protection.

FAQ

Common Questions About Epoxy Floors

How long does epoxy flooring last?

With proper installation and maintenance, epoxy flooring typically lasts 10-20 years in industrial environments. In lighter commercial or residential settings, it can last even longer. Key factors are substrate preparation quality and choosing the right system for your specific conditions.

How long before we can use the floor after installation?

Light foot traffic is typically possible after 24 hours. Full mechanical loading (forklifts, heavy equipment) requires 7 days for standard systems. Fast-cure formulations can reduce this significantly when downtime is critical.

Can epoxy be applied over existing tiles or coatings?

In most cases, yes — but it depends on the condition of the existing surface. Old coatings must be tested for adhesion and compatibility. Tiles can be overlaid if they're well-bonded and don't have excessive moisture. We assess each situation during site inspection.

Is epoxy flooring slippery when wet?

Standard smooth epoxy can be slippery when wet. For areas with water, oils, or other liquids, we incorporate anti-slip aggregates (quartz, aluminum oxide) into the system. Slip ratings from R9 to R13 are achievable depending on requirements.

What causes epoxy floors to fail?

The most common causes are: poor substrate preparation (80% of failures), moisture issues in the concrete, incorrect system selection for the environment, and applying over contaminated surfaces. This is why we invest heavily in surface preparation and testing.

Can damaged epoxy floors be repaired?

Yes. Localized damage can be repaired by grinding out the affected area and patching with compatible materials. For extensive wear, a full recoat can restore the floor to new condition. Repairs are one of epoxy's advantages over alternatives.
